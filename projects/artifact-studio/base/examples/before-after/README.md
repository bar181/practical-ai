# Before and after: one lesson, three ways

Bradley Ross, the author of Artifact Studio, needed a short lesson for a client's course: *Treat AI
like a smart intern.* He wrote the content once, then made three versions in new sessions. All
three hold the same facts. Only the design and the voice change.

| Version | File | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before | [`1-plain.md`](1-plain.md) | The plain version: a Markdown document. Clear and correct, and it looks like every other AI answer |
| After | [`2-teacher.html`](2-teacher.html) | A scrolling lesson in the teacher voice: a short robot clip, a vague brief beside a full one in tabs, frosted cards over painted scenes, the word "Launch" filled with a picture, check-yourself questions |
| After | [`3-story.html`](3-story.html) | The same lesson told as a story in seven beats: a Monday-morning opening, a candlelit look taken from the pictures, slow arrivals as you scroll |
| Source | [`source-content.md`](source-content.md) | The content all three were made from, with its rule: no facts beyond these |

## Honest notes

- The client is not named. The organisation's name is removed from all four files. Nothing else
  in the pages is changed. Bradley is named because he wrote them.
- The pictures and the clip were made with AI tools.
- The story's opening scene ("Monday, 8:40 a.m. Your desk") is framing the AI wrote. It is not in
  the source.
- The quote "The truth is the same, only the script differs" has no source. The plain version
  marks it `[needs: source or attribution]`. The two pages show it as the caption of a picture,
  with no attribution.
- Both pages load their fonts from Google Fonts. With no network they fall back to system fonts.

## Try it yourself

Paste the prompt below into the chatbot you use, with `source-content.md` under it. Attach your own
pictures, or leave them out. The request line is a suggestion; Bradley's sessions used the same
source content. To see the "before", send the source content alone.

```
Use the Artifact Studio design guide at
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bar181/practical-ai/main/projects/artifact-studio/base/AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md
Make this a scrolling lesson in the teacher voice, with the pictures I attach.
Then make a second version that tells it as a story.
```

The full prompt, ready to copy, is on the [Artifact Studio page](../../../README.md#try-it-yourself).
