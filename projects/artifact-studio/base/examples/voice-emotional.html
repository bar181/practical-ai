Behind this page

The personal story voice sells a feeling and lets the offer arrive last. Inspired by the classic advertising pitch that tells one personal story and never lists a feature. The narrator, the mother and the drawer are invented for the demo. When you use this voice, the memory has to be a true one of yours.

How it is built

  1. One memory, in the first person, in a single short line
  2. Two or three keepsakes, each with a caption a person would write by hand
  3. One photograph, the whole screen, moving slowly, with one line over it
  4. The feeling, named in one sentence
  5. The turn: what has changed, and what has not
  6. What to do, in one line
  7. The offer, named once, on the last screen

Design rules

  • A dark, warm room. Serif type, mostly italic, never bold
  • Photographs faded and warm, a little crooked, with a paper border
  • Never more than two sentences on a screen
  • Everything arrives slowly. Nothing bounces
  • No features, no numbers, no charts
  • The name of the product appears once, small, at the end
