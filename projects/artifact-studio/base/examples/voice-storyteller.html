- What this does not show: one company, no comparison group, spring is the busy season, and jobs won are not counted yet

# Delivery note

The page is `out.html`. It is a draft, not a finished page: your photo was not attached, so the large photo space is empty and the colours are a stand-in.

## Chose

- **Kind of thing:** a page to be read, because you said "on one page".
- **Recipe:** landing page. It is the only recipe that lists the storyteller voice, and it fits "one kind of visitor should take one action": the owners, deciding by 26 May.
- **Voice:** storyteller. You asked for a story with your customer as the hero and you as the guide. The voice set the order, so the page runs in eight named beats: hook, hero, problem at three depths, guide (empathy then proof), three-step plan, one call to action, failure beside success, an ending that returns to the Tuesday evening.
- **Look:** storyteller default is "colours from the photograph". With no photograph, I used a provisional set: pale plaster background, blue-black text, one lamp-amber accent for the button only, one teal data colour for the charts. Large system serif. Beat names in small italic type.
- **Parts:** three stat rows (number, what it was before), two thin bar charts (site visits by month; 26 hours against 38 minutes drawn to scale), three questions that open on tap, a before and during pair of lists, one button shown twice. One moving part: a slow push-in on the photo and on the two promise lines.
- **Where the landing page sections went:** promise with a number (start of beat 4 and again in beat 8), picture of the result (the site visits chart), three things you get (beat 4), three steps and three questions (beat 5), price and button (beat 6), promise and button again (beat 8).

- **Design skill:** the guide says to load a front-end design skill if one is available. One was, so I loaded it. Where it disagreed with the guide, the guide won.
