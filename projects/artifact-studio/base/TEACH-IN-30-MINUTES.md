# Teach Artifact Studio in 30 minutes

A lesson plan for one teacher and any number of learners. Nobody needs to code. Each learner
needs a chatbot they already use, and some notes of their own: a report, meeting notes, a plan.

**By the end** each learner has turned their own notes into a page or a deck they would be
willing to send, and can ask for the next one in a single sentence.

**Think of it as** a cooking class. You show the dish, explain one idea, and then everyone cooks
their own.

## Before the lesson

| Do this | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Open [the showcase page](https://bar181.github.io/practical-ai/artifact-studio/showcase.html) in a browser on the screen you will present from. Offline? Download [`showcase.html`](../showcase.html) | 1 minute |
| Download [`examples/voice-explainer.html`](examples/voice-explainer.html) too. It is a lesson on this tool, made by an AI from a prompt | 1 minute |
| Have the "Try it" prompt from the [README](../README.md#try-it-now) ready to share | 1 minute |
| Ask each learner to bring notes they do not mind showing: nothing private | The day before |

## The lesson

| Minutes | You do | You say | Learners do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 3 | Open the showcase page on the Photo effects tab. Scroll slowly | "This is one picture and one sentence each time. Your AI can make this. It needs to be told how." | Watch |
| 3 to 7 | Open the Visionary tab. Scroll the first three screens | "AI has read every book in the world. It has never met you. Think of it as a smart intern: it needs a brief." | Tell the person beside them one thing a new intern would get wrong on day one |
| 7 to 10 | Open the Explainer tab, at "Build a good ask". Press Step six times | "The job, the reader, the shape. Those three change the page more than anything else." | Write their own ask: job, reader, shape |
| 10 to 20 | Share the prompt. Walk the room | "Paste it in, then your notes, then your ask. If it shows code and not a page, say: show this as an artifact, or ask for a preview." | Make their first page from their own notes |
| 20 to 25 | Open the Cinematic effects tab in the showcase | "One or two effects make a page feel directed. All eight make it a showreel." | Ask for the same content a second way: as a deck, or with one cinematic effect added |
| 25 to 30 | Show the delivery note on one learner's screen | "It tells you what it assumed and what to check. The AI builds the file. You sign it." | Check one number on their page against their notes. Say what they would change |

## What to watch for

| You see | It means | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Make it look better" typed into the chat | The old habit | "Better for whom? Name the reader." |
| A number on the page that is not in the notes | The AI filled a gap | "Have it removed. The instructions tell it to mark the gap, not guess." |
| Code on the screen where a page should be | A bare HTML file | "Ask for an artifact or a preview." |
| A learner with no notes | It happens | Point them to a [worked example](examples/): each has sample notes to copy |
| The chatbot cannot open the link | Common | "Open [`AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md`](AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md), copy all of it, paste it in first." |

## After the lesson

Ask each learner to set it up once so they never paste it again: a Project in Claude or ChatGPT,
or a Gem in Gemini, with the instructions pasted into it. Then ask them to send you one page they
made for real work.

This plan was written with the project and has not yet been taught. The timings are estimates.
It has been run with Claude only: try your own chatbot before the class.
