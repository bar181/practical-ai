# Three ways to tailor it for your business

1. **Make it look like yours.** Send one brand colour, or a photo of a finished job. From one colour I can build the full set of seven page colours and list their contrast. From a photo I can take the colours out of the picture and let it fill the opening screen, which the keynote voice allows. Add your company name and the year at the same time and the deck stops being anonymous.

2. **Point it at a different reader.** The same six facts can be re-cut for whoever has to act on them:
   - for your crews: this deck, plus the next step you want from them;
   - for a bank, a partner or your own records: a one-page report in the analyst or executive brief voice, with the working shown;
   - for customers: a landing page or a square social post built on the one promise in your numbers, a quote within two days of the visit. That one needs you to confirm you are willing to promise it publicly.

3. **Fill the gaps and make it monthly.** Tell me the next step, and give me the split by crew or by service, and the deck gains its missing slide and the report gains its table with the weak row marked. Add three or four earlier months and the two bars become a real trend line. If you write your choices (voice, colours, company name, who it is for) into a short preferences note and send it with each month's numbers, every month's deck comes out the same way without you repeating yourself.
