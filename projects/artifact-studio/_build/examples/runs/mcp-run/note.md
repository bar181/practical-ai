## What I assumed, because I could not ask

1. **A page, not slides.** "Something I can show" could be either. The tools scored a one-page
   report highest for "say how something went" to "my own team". A briefing deck was the second
   suggestion and is a fair alternative if you will be standing at a screen.
2. **It speaks as "we".** The page is written as you talking to your two leads.
3. **No company name.** You did not give one, so there is none on the page.
4. **"Up from 38%" and "used to take six" are not tied to September.** Your notes do not say which
   period those older figures are from, so the page does not say either.
5. **The van and the quote change are not dated.** The notes do not say when in the month, so the
   page says "four days" and "now" and no more.
6. **No owner beside the November steps.** The analyst voice asks for an owner and a date for each
   next step. You gave dates but no names, so the table has "Step" and "By" only.

## What the recipe asked for that your notes could not fill

- **A trend chart.** The recipe wants a trend line. Two months is not a trend, so the chart is two
  bars, September in grey and October in colour, and the page says so under "What this page does
  not show".
- **A breakdown table with the weak row marked.** There is no breakdown in the notes (by crew, by
  job type, by week), so that part is left out. Inventing rows was not an option.
- **How it was measured.** Left out, because the notes do not say.

## Things for you to check

- **The 15 November date.** The computer I ran on says today is Monday 16 November 2026. If that is
  the Monday you mean, the van deadline was yesterday. I kept your date as written and put no year
  or weekday on the page. Change the row if the decision has been made or the date has moved.
- **"So what: about 18% more than September."** True by arithmetic. Remove it if you would rather
  not put a percentage in front of the crew.
- **"What this page does not show."** It is honest and the voice asks for it, but it is the most
  office-like part of the page. Delete that card if it is wrong for the room; nothing else depends
  on it.
- **The page does not say why more quotes became jobs.** Faster quotes and a higher win rate sit
  side by side, and people will connect them. Your notes do not claim the link, so the page does
  not either. Say it yourself on Monday if you believe it.
