Trial report for the owners. February to April, 90 days, all 214 enquiries.

With drafted replies, our first reply went from 26 hours to 38 minutes and site visits rose

During the 90-day trial the median first reply fell from 26 hours to 38 minutes, site visits booked rose from 18 a month to 31, and the cost is about $1,800 a year. That evidence favours keeping the drafted replies after 31 May, but it comes from one company in its busy season with no comparison group, and jobs won are not counted yet.

Decision needed from the owners by 26 May. The trial ends on 31 May.

Speed: 81% of enquiries were answered within the hour, up from 12%

So what: the typical enquiry used to wait more than a day for a first reply. It now waits 38 minutes, and a member of staff still reads, fixes and sends every reply.

Visits: site visits booked rose each month, from 18 a month to 31

So what: the trial months booked 81 visits (22 + 28 + 31) against 54 at the earlier rate (18 × 3), 27 more. At about one visit in four, that would be about 7 more jobs (27 ÷ 4 = 6.75). This is an estimate: jobs won are not counted yet, and spring is the busy season.

Cost: about $1,800 a year, against about $26,000 for a part-time coordinator

So what: about $150 a month is about $1,800 a year (150 × 12), which is about $24,200 a year less than a coordinator (26,000 − 1,800). The office manager's time on replies also fell from 6 hours a week to 2.5, which gives back 3.5 hours a week (6 − 2.5).

Method: we used it on every enquiry for 90 days, and a person approved every reply

The trial ran from February to April and covered all 214 enquiries, which arrive by web form, email and telephone. What changed: a reply is drafted the moment a message arrives, then a member of staff reads it, fixes it and sends it.

The 26 hours and 12% are from before the trial. [needs: the dates and the number of enquiries those two before figures cover]

Our rules for the trial:

  • A person approves every reply.
  • The first reply confirms and offers a visit.
  • It never states a price.

Setting it up took about an hour:

  1. Collected three replies we are proud of.
  2. Wrote our rules on half a page.
  3. Saved both in a chat project.
  4. Tested it on five old enquiries.
  5. Used it on the next real one.
