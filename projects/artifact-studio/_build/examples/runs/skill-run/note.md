# Delivery note: Saturday opening deck

File: `out.html`. Six slides, one self-contained file, system fonts, no network requests, no images.
Arrow keys, the buttons, a tap or a swipe move between slides. `N` shows a speaker's note for each slide. Printing gives one slide per page.
I could not show it as a rendered preview here, so open the file in a browser.

## What I chose

- **Recipe: briefing deck**, built from the skill's `deck-briefing.html` starter. You asked for a deck and the job is a decision, so I took the deck made for a decision. Runner-up: the Proposal page recipe, which has the same job ("turn a conversation into a decision") but is a page for a customer with prices and terms, which your notes do not have.
- **Voice: executive brief.** The voice card for "make a decision in three minutes". It set the slide order: header, the decision, background, options in one table including "do nothing", recommendation in two sentences, risks with their control, next step.
- **Look: paper memo**, the default look for the executive brief voice. White slides on a grey surround, serif text, sans headings and labels, one dark blue for rules, labels and the one action. No motion.
- **Parts:** one numbered list, one comparison table with the recommended row marked in words, one list of unknowns. No chart, no moving parts.
- **Preferences applied:** none. No preferences file came with the request.

## What I assumed

1. The audience is the partners, the job is a yes to the trial, and it will be shown on a meeting screen on Thursday. A phone is the second place it will be read.
2. The decision is needed at Thursday's meeting. Your notes say the deck is for that meeting, not that the decision has to be made there.
3. The exact ask is "approve an eight-week trial of Saturday opening, 9 to 1". Your notes say you want the partners to agree to Saturday opening and that you want an eight-week trial. I joined the two.
4. 8 appointments means 8 for each Saturday, and there is one Saturday a week.
5. Saturday staff hours are 12 a week. This is my sum: 8 physiotherapist hours + 4 reception hours. The working is shown in the table cell.
6. The "Do nothing" row is mine. The voice requires it. "None" and "None" are true by definition and are not from your notes.
7. "The control for both: this is an eight-week trial, not a permanent change." Your notes list two things you are not sure of and then ask for a trial. I assumed the trial is your answer to both. The notes do not say so.
8. "The two who volunteered" is kept in your words. The notes do not say they are the two physiotherapists, so the deck does not say it either.
9. "58 of 90 patients surveyed": I assumed 90 is the number who answered the spring survey.
10. I did not turn 58 of 90 into a percentage, and I did not multiply anything across the eight weeks. Every other number is copied from your notes.
11. "Four physiotherapists" is not on a slide. The partners know it and it does not help the decision.
12. The speaker's notes are my wording. They are advice on delivery and contain no new facts about the clinic.
13. The header has no "From" line and no date, because your name and the date of the meeting were not in the notes.

## What is missing

Marked on the page:

- **How many appointments the later-evenings option would add.** The options table shows `[needs: number of appointments]` in that cell. Without it the partners cannot compare the two options on the same question.

Not marked on the page, but the partners are likely to ask:

- **Why Saturday and not later evenings.** Saturday takes 12 staff hours a week against about 6. The notes give no reason for preferring it beyond the survey. This is the weakest point in the deck.
- What a Saturday costs (pay, opening the building) and what 8 appointments bring in. There is no money anywhere in the notes.
- The start date of the trial.
- How the trial will be judged: what counts as success, and how you will tell new patients from weekday patients moving.
- What happens if one of the two volunteers stops during the eight weeks.
- Your name and the date of Thursday's meeting, for the header.
- How many of the 41 on the waiting list want a weekend and how many want an evening.

## What to check before sharing

1. Read every number against your notes: three weeks, 41, 58 of 90, 9 to 1, two physiotherapists, 8 hours, 4 hours, 8 appointments, about 6, eight weeks.
2. Slide 3: either fill the `[needs: ...]` cell or decide to say aloud that it is not known. Do not present with the bracket showing unless you mean to.
3. Slide 3: confirm that 12 staff hours a week is the right way to state the Saturday cost, and have an answer ready for "why not evenings at half the hours".
4. Slide 5: confirm that the eight-week trial really is your control for both unknowns. If you plan to measure something, say what.
5. Slide 1: confirm the decision is wanted on Thursday and not later.
6. Open it on the screen you will present from, press Full screen, and step through once. Then open it once on a phone.
