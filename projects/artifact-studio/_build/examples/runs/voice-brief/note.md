## Chose

- **Kind of thing:** a page to be read, because you asked for a one-page brief.
- **Recipe:** one-page report. No recipe is a decision brief, and the guide says to use the one-page report when none fits. The proposal recipe is for a customer saying yes, which this is not.
- **Voice:** executive brief. You asked for "a one-page decision brief for busy executives" in the order decision, background, options, recommendation, risks. That is this voice's order. The voice adds a memo header and next steps.
- **Look:** the voice's default, a paper memo. Serif system type, one accent (blue, used only on the deadline and the recommended option), no images, no motion.
- **Parts:** three numbers with what they were before, one thin bar chart of site visits booked, one options table. No moving parts.
- **Colours and contrast:** main text on paper 15.3 to 1. Secondary text on paper 6.7 to 1. Accent on paper 8.0 to 1. Main text on the highlighted row 13.9 to 1. Accent on the highlighted row 7.3 to 1. Chart bars on paper 5.5 to 1.
- **Preferences applied:** no preferences file came with the request. The only standing writing preference I had was "no em-dashes", and the page and these notes follow it.
