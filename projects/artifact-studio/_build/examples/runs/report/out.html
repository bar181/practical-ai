Method

Every figure comes from our own notes, and each change is October minus the earlier figure.

  • Revenue: $84,200 − $71,500 = $12,700. Then $12,700 ÷ $71,500 = 17.8%, shown as about 18%.
  • Jobs completed: 23 − 19 = 4.
  • Quotes that became jobs: 46% − 38% = 8 percentage points.

Limits

Two months make a comparison, not a trend, and the notes leave some things out.

  • There are only two months of figures. That is enough to compare, not enough to call a trend.
  • The notes have no costs or profit, so this report cannot say whether October made more money after costs.
  • The notes have no split by crew or by type of job, so the table compares measures and does not break revenue down.
  • The notes do not say which month the 38% and the six days are from.
  • There is no earlier figure for van days off the road.
