## Three ways to tailor this for your business

1. **Use your real green.** Send a colour code, or a photo of your van or logo, and the seven colours are rebuilt from it so the page matches your signs and quotes.
2. **Save your defaults once.** Keep a short `preferences.md` that says: one-page report, analyst voice, your green, partner reads on a phone. Each month you then paste new notes and get the same shape, so November sits beside October. Add a split by crew or by type of job and a third month, and the table becomes a real breakdown and the chart a real trend.
3. **Pick a different recipe or voice for a different reader.** For the van decision, ask for the executive brief voice: it leads with the decision and lays out the options in one table, including "do nothing". For customers, ask for the proposal recipe and your quotes go out as a page with three options, one marked, and a schedule. That fits the two-day quote habit you have just started.
