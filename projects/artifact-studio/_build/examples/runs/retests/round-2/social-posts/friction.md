# Friction with the guide

Twelve problems. Four made me guess about something that changes the page.

1. **BLOCKING. The feed post needs a name and nothing says what to do when the notes have none.**
   Line: "a feed post (name, the text cut after about three lines with a control to read the rest, ...)".
   The person's message gives no name, only a GitHub handle inside a link. The gap rule ("If the page would mislead without it, a number, a name, a date or a price the reader will act on, mark it on the page as `[needs: ...]`. Otherwise leave the page clean") does not settle it: an author name is "a name", but the reader does not "act on" it and the page does not mislead without it. I could have left the slot out, written "Your name", or marked it. I marked it `[needs: your name]` three times, which is the loudest thing on the page. The guide should say what a required frame slot shows when the content cannot fill it.

2. **BLOCKING. A post has no look.**
   Lines: "For a post, an email or a blog, the voice is the person's own." and "4. **Look.** In order: the person's design spec; colours taken from a picture, only when the person asks for its colours or it is the page's main picture; the voice's default look."
   Step 4 ends at "the voice's default look". The person's own voice has no row in the voices table and no entry under "Defaults by voice". The only hint is "a plain frame" in the post preview paragraph, which says nothing about the page around the frames, the type or the accent. I invented all seven colours. Two assistants would hand back two different pages here.

3. **BLOCKING. "About three lines" is not a rule I can build to, and it may not be where the feed cuts.**
   Line: "the text cut after about three lines with a control to read the rest".
   The person asked for "the text cut where the feed would cut it". The guide answers with "about". It does not say three lines at what width and type size, whether a blank line counts as a line, or whether the cut is by lines or by characters. Those choices decide what sits above the cut, and I wrote all three openings to fit it. I guessed: exactly three rendered lines, 14 pixel type, a frame about 330 to 400 pixels wide, blank lines count. Rule 3 of the test told me not to correct the guide from my own knowledge, so the page may not match the real feed, and I had to tell the person so under "Check before sharing".

4. **BLOCKING. Nothing says which picture goes with which draft.**
   Line: "one picture for each draft when there are several".
   Three pictures, three drafts, no pairing rule and no instruction to pair by meaning or by the order given. I paired by meaning (bored call with the problem, dog with the intern, lightbulb with the first step). Pairing by the order in the notes gives the same result here only by luck. The phrase is also ambiguous: "when there are several" could mean several drafts or several pictures. If the person had sent one picture, I would not know whether to repeat it or leave two drafts bare.

5. **MINOR. The phone rule contradicts itself within two sentences.**
   Lines: "'Side by side' means side by side on a wide screen and a row to swipe, or a stack, on a phone." then "Nothing scrolls sideways except a table inside its own container."
   A row to swipe scrolls sideways and is not a table. I chose the stack because it satisfies both lines. The swipe option is dead as written.

6. **MINOR. "The actions as plain words" does not say which words or what they are.**
   Line: "the actions as plain words".
   I guessed Like, Comment, Repost, Send. The checklist says "Every button does something", so I made them plain text and not buttons, but the guide never says so. Using the platform's own action names also sits close to "Never draw a platform's logo or copy its interface". Where the line is, I cannot tell.

7. **MINOR. I could not place this request in the request table, and the answer changes the note.**
   Lines: "'Use the artifact-studio skill' (expert) | Every step, the check, and the note in full" and "Anything in between | Treat it as a one-sentence request and follow whatever it names".
   The person wrote "Read the design guide at the link and follow it". In the single-file edition that is the only way to call the guide at all, so it could be the expert trigger or it could be nothing. I treated it as "in between". Then the short note ("one line under each is enough") collides with the checklist line "Every gap is listed in the note" and with "Wording, structure and an analogy are yours to write: say so under Assumed". I had about nine assumptions. One line would not hold them, so my note is longer than the table asks for.

8. **MINOR. No pattern is given for "a control to read the rest" that works with scripts off.**
   Lines: "Build tabs from radio buttons and labels so they switch with scripts off" and "Real controls for interaction: `button`, `input`, `details`, `select`."
   The guide gives a scripts-off pattern for tabs only. `details` cannot show the first three lines and hide the rest, and a `button` needs a script. I used a hidden checkbox and a label, which the list allows as an `input`, but a checkbox announced as "Read the rest, not checked" is an odd control. The guide should name the pattern it wants.

9. **MINOR. "Moving parts" is never defined.**
   Line: "At most three moving parts on a page."
   I have three identical "Read the rest" controls. I do not know whether that is three moving parts, one, or none because nothing animates.

10. **MINOR. The outline step does not work for a post.**
    Line: "5. **Outline.** The voice gives the order. The recipe is a checklist of what a page of that kind usually holds."
    A post has no recipe and the person's own voice has no order. Nothing says what the page around the previews holds: a title, an intro line, a reminder that nothing was posted. I added a short heading and one sentence on my own judgement.

11. **MINOR. The image rule and the "shape its audience will see" rule pull apart.**
    Lines: "Images: alt text that says what is shown, cropped to the subject at phone width" and "Show each draft in a plain frame in the shape its audience will see", plus, under Cards, "The same padding and the same order in every card of a row."
    The three pictures have three shapes (wide, less wide, square). Cropping them to one shape makes the row even and breaks the feed's look. Leaving them whole keeps the feed's look and makes three frames of three heights. I left them whole.

12. **MINOR. Nothing says whether a link inside a previewed post is live.**
    Line: none. The post preview paragraph is silent, and "Every button does something, and it works by keyboard" is the nearest rule.
    A live link below the cut still takes keyboard focus while it is hidden, which breaks keyboard use. A styled but dead link breaks "every button does something". I showed the address as plain text.

Also noted, not counted: "Name the platform in words above the frame" and "name the way in above each" put two labels above each frame and give no order. I put the way in first because it is what the person is choosing between.

Blocking: 4. Minor: 8.
