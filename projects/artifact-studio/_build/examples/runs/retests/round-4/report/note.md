## Three ways to tailor this for your own business

1. **Give your real brand, not the word "green".** Send your hex code, your company name and your logo. The seven colours get rebuilt from your exact green and the page carries your name.
2. **Save your standing choices once.** Keep a short `preferences.md` (or a Preferences section) that says, for example: recipe one-page report, analyst voice, your colours, reader on a phone, answer first. Each month you paste the new notes and the report comes out the same way without repeating yourself.
3. **Feed it the detail that turns a comparison into a report.** Add revenue and jobs split by crew or by type of job (that fills the breakdown table and marks the weak row), three or more months of figures (two bars become a real trend), and an owner for each next step. For the van, add the cost and the options you are weighing, including doing nothing, and ask for an executive brief: that gives your partner a one-sheet decision memo with an options table.
