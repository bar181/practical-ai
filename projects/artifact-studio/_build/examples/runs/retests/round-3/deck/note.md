1. **Your colours.** Give me one brand colour, a design spec, or a main photo whose colours you want. The deck is black and white only because I had nothing to go on. From one brand colour I can build the full set of seven and report the contrast of each pair.
2. **Your pictures.** Send one or two photos of finished jobs. In this voice a picture fills a whole slide or sits beside one short paragraph, so a single strong before-and-after of a garden would do more than any extra number.
3. **Your audience and your ask.** Tell me who will watch and what you want them to do next. For your crews, the closing slide becomes the next rule to try. For a lender or a partner, the same numbers work better as a briefing deck (answer first, reasons, options, decision). For customers, a story deck with the customer as the hero.
