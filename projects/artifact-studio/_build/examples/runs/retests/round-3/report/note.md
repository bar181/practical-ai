# Delivery note: October report

The page is `out.html`. Open it in a browser: a bare HTML file can open as code on some phones.

## Chose

- **Kind and recipe:** a page to be read, using the one-page report, because you asked for a one-page report. The van decision gets its own section inside the report.
- **Voice:** analyst, the report's first voice. It puts the conclusion in the title and the answer in two sentences, which is what your partner asked for.
- **Look:** quiet, built from your green. Green is the one data colour in the charts, and the comparison bars are grey.
- **Parts:** three number tiles (revenue the largest), two thin two-bar charts, one decision panel. No moving parts and no script.
- **Preferences:** no preferences file was supplied, so none were applied.

## Three ways to tailor this for your own business

1. **Give your real brand details once.** Send the exact colour code for your green and your company name. The page then uses your green in place of the one I picked, and the name goes in the header.
2. **Keep a short preferences file.** A file named `preferences.md` with a few lines such as "reader is my partner, on her phone", "answer first", "one-page report" and your colour. The guide reads it before choosing anything, so each month you paste only the new notes.
3. **Add the facts the page had to leave out.** A split by crew gives you a breakdown table with the weaker row marked. An owner for each next step replaces the `[needs: owner]` marks. Options and costs for the van, including "do nothing", let you ask for a decision memo in place of a report, with one options table your partner can answer in three minutes.
