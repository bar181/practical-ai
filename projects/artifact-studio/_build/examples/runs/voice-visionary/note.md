# Delivery note: Practical AI keynote page

File: `out.html` (one file, about 330 KB, pictures embedded, system fonts, no network requests). Open it in a browser to see it rendered. Many phones show a bare HTML file as code.

I chose a page, not a slide deck, because you asked for a "page". It is read by scrolling and each screen snaps into place.

## Chose

- **Recipe: scrolling explainer.** You named no recipe. Your notes explain an idea, and keynote is one of that recipe's listed voices.
- **Voice: keynote.** You named it ("keynote-style"). The content follows its order: the world as it is, the catch, the idea, proof as numbers, the next step, a closing line.
- **Look: black and white, one typeface.** This is the keynote look. One grey (`#adbabd`) is sampled from the fog picture and used for small type only. No accent colour, because the page has nothing to click.
- **17 screens**, one idea each, 4 to 10 words on every screen (twelve is the limit).

## Assumed

I could not ask questions, so I decided these myself.

1. **Audience:** people who might try Practical AI, seeing the page on a phone or on a shared screen while you talk. You did not say who it is for.
2. **The one job:** get the reader to take the first step (paste, ask, check).
3. **"Keynote-style" is your design direction.** So the page is black, as the keynote voice says, and I did not build the palette from your pictures. The guide ranks picture colours above a voice's default look, and its picture rule would have given a pale page. I judged that is not what "keynote-style" means.
4. **A "stage" is a keynote beat, not a screen.** The recipe allows seven stages at most. The page has six beats spread over 17 screens. Your notes hold more than seven ideas, so "one idea per screen" could not fit in seven screens.
5. **Which picture goes with which line** was my choice. You did not say. In particular, I put the city beside "every day, every week, every month" because of the loop shape in its sky.
6. **Numbers count as words.** That is why the six numbers are split over two screens of three.
7. **The marker on screen 13 counts as words too.** That screen is at ten.
8. **The three start steps get one screen each**, because the keynote voice avoids bullet lists.
9. **"About five minutes" is shown in words at normal size**, with "My estimate." under it, not as an enormous number. It is an estimate, and enormous numbers are for proof.
10. **Wording was shortened, nothing added.** For example "a free, open set of tools" became "free, open tools", and "The idea I teach:" was dropped. "Judgement" keeps your spelling.
11. **The title "Practical AI" sits small at the top left of screen 1**, and the first big line is the world as it is. The recipe wants a title first. The voice wants the world first.
12. **No total is shown** for the six library numbers. You did not give one, and the page does not need one.
13. **The city is not named** in the picture description. Your message calls it "a city at sunset".
14. **The pictures were embedded as supplied.** I checked all three: none carries location data or any camera data.
