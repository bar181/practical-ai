I am teaching a class how to use Artifact Studio. This is the procedure.

1. Copy the instructions file, base/AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md. You never need to read it
2. Paste it where your assistant keeps instructions: a Project in Claude or ChatGPT, a Gem in Gemini. With no Project, paste it as the first message of a chat
3. Ask in one sentence. Name the job, the reader and the shape. Attach your notes
4. Check what comes back: open it on a phone, check every number and name against your notes, and read the list of what it assumed

- The analogy I use: AI is a smart intern who has read every book in the world and has never met you. You would hand an intern a brief, not a pile of notes
- A good ask, one line at a time: Turn my October notes into a one-page report / for my business partner, who reads on her phone. / She wants the answer first. / Use the analyst voice. / Our colour is green. / Tell me what you assumed.
- Exercise, five minutes: take the last thing you asked an AI to write, add the reader and the shape, ask again, and compare the two
- Two pictures attached: a cartoon of a bored audience on a video call, and a delighted cat
