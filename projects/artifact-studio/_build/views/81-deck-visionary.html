The visionary deck

Nine slides in the keynote manner: black, huge type, one idea each, and three pictures with a few words over them. Tap the right side of a slide to move on, or use the arrow keys.

Behind this deck

The visionary deck is for a room. It makes people feel that something has changed, and it leaves the detail for the conversation afterwards. It follows the manner of Steve Jobs's product launches: a picture, a villain, one rule, three things, one number, and a quiet last slide. The company and figures are invented.

How it is built

  1. Open on the feeling, in one sentence
  2. A full-screen picture with one line over it
  3. Name the villain as a single number
  4. Say what the villain cost
  5. State the idea as one rule
  6. Three things, three words
  7. The proof: one number, with the old one struck through
  8. What the proof bought
  9. "One more thing": the easiest possible next step

Design rules

  • Black background, white type, one grey for everything secondary
  • One idea on each slide, and never more than about ten words
  • Numbers larger than anything else on the screen
  • No bullet lists, no logo on every slide, no footer
  • Three pictures in nine slides, each filling the slide, each with a few words over it. Never a picture beside a paragraph
  • The speaker carries the detail. The slide carries the beat. Each slide has a note for the speaker: tap Notes or press N
