Behind this page

A proposal turns a conversation into a decision. It repeats what the customer asked for, shows the answer, prices it three ways and makes yes easy. This one uses the practical voice with a calm look.

Recipe: the order of the page

  1. Cover: the promise, who it is for, how long it is valid
  2. What you asked for, in the customer's words
  3. What we propose, with one drawing
  4. Three options, with the suggested one marked
  5. The schedule
  6. What is included and what is not
  7. Terms
  8. One button

Components used

  • A line drawing that draws the new parts
  • Price cards
  • A schedule chart
  • Old way, new way lists, used for included and not included

Goes well with

  • Voices: the practical instructor, the executive brief
  • Looks: calm, two colours, a serif for the big words
