The briefing deck

Six slides for a decision: the answer first, then the reasons, the numbers, the options, what is not known and what you need from the room. Tap the right side of a slide to move on.

The recommendation

Keep the one-hour rule. Extend it to weekends.

It costs four hours a week and wins thirteen more site visits a month.

Say the answer in the first ten seconds. If they stop you here and agree, you are done.

Behind this deck

The briefing deck is for a room that has to decide. It gives the answer on the first slide, so the meeting can end early, and it says what is not known before anyone asks. The company and figures are invented.

How it is built

  1. The recommendation, in one sentence
  2. Three reasons
  3. Two numbers, each with what it was before
  4. Every option on one table, with the recommended one marked
  5. What is not known
  6. The decision and the date

Design rules

  • Light background, dark ink, one blue. Nothing decorative
  • Left-aligned, with a small label naming each slide’s job
  • No more than three reasons and three options
  • Numbers always carry what they were before
  • Each slide has a note for the speaker: tap Notes or press N
