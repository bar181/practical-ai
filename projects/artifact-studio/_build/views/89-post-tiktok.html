TikTok: three beats, nine seconds

A short video is a storyboard before it is a video. See the three frames side by side and you can fix the hook before you film anything.

The storyboard

One frame per beat, with the words that will be on screen. The moving version is on the Three drafts tab.

Beat 1: the hookSay what happened

0 to 3 seconds

You asked AI for a report.

Beat 2: the turnName the problem

3 to 6 seconds

It came back grey.

Beat 3: the payoffShow the result

6 to 9 seconds

Then you gave it a brief.

What you say

"You asked an AI for a report and it came back as a grey wall of text. The thinking was fine. Nobody told it who the page was for. Give it a brief and the same notes come back like this. The brief is free. Link in my profile."

What to check in this preview

  • Does the first frame make sense with the sound off?
  • Are the words on screen seven or fewer per beat?
  • Is the payoff a picture, not a sentence?
  • Is the ask in the last two seconds?

Ask for it like this

Storyboard a nine-second TikTok from my notes: three beats, the words on screen for each, and what I say. Show the three frames side by side at phone shape, using my photos.
