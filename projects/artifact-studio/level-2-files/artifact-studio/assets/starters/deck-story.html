The person

She wants a kitchen she is glad to come home to.

Start with her, not with us. Use the words a customer would use.

The problem

She asked three companies. Nobody wrote back for a day.

It is a small thing, and it tells her how the whole job will go.

Name what happened, then how it felt. The feeling is the real problem.

The guide

We have been the company that wrote back late.

Our replies took 26 hours. We changed one rule, and now they take 38 minutes.

Now we may enter, as the guide. One line of empathy, one proof.

The plan

Three steps, and the first is free.

  1. Tell us about the room. We answer within the hour.
  2. We visit, measure and listen.
  3. You get one clear price with three options.
Three steps, and make the first one cost nothing.

The ask

Send us two photos of your kitchen today.

Start with two photos
Ask for one thing only. Say it the same way the button says it.

If nothing changes

Another winter of the drawer that sticks and the light you never liked.

Say what stays the same if she does nothing. Keep it gentle and true.

How it ends

Friends in the kitchen, and you not apologising for it.

Close on the picture of life afterwards, and return to the opening image.
