Behind this page

The keynote voice sells a vision by removing everything else. Inspired by Steve Jobs's product launches. This page is about the Practical AI repository itself. The six counts are checked against the library every time it is built, and five minutes is an estimate, not a measurement.

How it is built

  1. The world as it is, in one line of huge type
  2. The catch, said quietly
  3. The consequence, over a picture that moves slower than the page
  4. The idea, with a few words beside one picture
  5. What it is for, under a small drawing that moves
  6. The proof, as a chart with no axes
  7. The next step, on frosted glass over a picture
  8. A closing line short enough to repeat

Design rules

  • One idea per screen. If it needs two sentences, it is two screens.
  • Black background, one typeface, white and grey only. The pictures bring the colour.
  • Three screens are nothing but type. They carry the argument.
  • A picture fills the screen or sits beside one short paragraph. Never both.
  • A chart has no axes or gridlines. The numbers are the labels.
  • Each screen snaps into place and its words arrive in order
