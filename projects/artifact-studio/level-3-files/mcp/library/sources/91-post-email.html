What to check in this preview

  • Would you open this from the subject and the preview line alone?
  • Is there one button, and does it say what happens?
  • Does the message still make sense with pictures turned off?
  • Is your name at the bottom, and a way to leave the list?

Ask for it like this

Write a short email newsletter from my notes in my voice. Show the inbox line first: sender, subject and preview text. Then the message as an HTML email at phone width, with one picture and one button.
