Behind this deck

The visionary deck is for a room. It makes people feel that something has changed, and it leaves the detail for the conversation afterwards. It follows the manner of Steve Jobs's product launches: a villain, one rule, three things, one number, and a quiet last slide. The company and figures are invented.

How it is built

  1. Open on the feeling, in one sentence
  2. Name the villain as a single number
  3. Say what the villain cost
  4. State the idea as one rule
  5. Three things, three words
  6. The proof: one number, with the old one struck through
  7. What the proof bought
  8. "One more thing": the easiest possible next step

Design rules

  • Black background, white type, one grey for everything secondary
  • One idea on each slide, and never more than about ten words
  • Numbers larger than anything else on the screen
  • No bullet lists, no logo on every slide, no footer
  • No pictures unless the picture is the idea
  • The speaker carries the detail. The slide carries the beat. Each slide has a note for the speaker: tap Notes or press N
