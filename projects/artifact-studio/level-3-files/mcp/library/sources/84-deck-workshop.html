Behind this deck

The workshop deck is for teaching a room one skill. People practise before the session ends: a picture to hold the idea, the steps, one worked example, then their turn. The company and figures are invented.

How it is built

  1. What they will be able to do, as one promise
  2. A picture or comparison to hold the idea
  3. Three steps
  4. One worked example, shown whole
  5. Their turn, with a time limit
  6. A check they can run on their own work
  7. A card to keep

Design rules

  • Warm white with one deep green. A yellow label names each slide’s job
  • The “your turn” slide changes colour, so the room knows to stop listening and start doing
  • Three steps at most. One example, not three
  • Every instruction is something a person can do
  • Each slide has a note for the speaker: tap Notes or press N
