First-reply trial, 90 days. Prepared for the owners.

Replying within the hour raised booked site visits by 72%. Make it the standard.

The answer

Adopt drafted first replies with human approval for all enquiries. The trial paid for itself in the first month and reduced staff time.

38 minmedian first reply, down from 26 h
31site visits a month, up from 18
2.5 hstaff time a week, down from 6 h

How we measured

  • All 214 enquiries received in the 90 days
  • Reply time taken from mailbox timestamps
  • Visits counted from the shared calendar

What this does not show

  • One company and no comparison group
  • Spring is the busy season, so some of the rise is seasonal
  • Jobs won lag visits by weeks. That number is not in yet.

Behind this page

The analyst voice gives the answer first and then earns it with evidence. Inspired by Barbara Minto's pyramid principle. The company and every figure are invented for the demo.

How it is built

  1. A title that is the conclusion, not the topic
  2. The answer in two sentences, on a glass card over one slow-moving picture
  3. Three numbers that carry it
  4. Three findings. Each heading is a full sentence, each has one chart and a "so what".
  5. How it was measured
  6. What the evidence does not show
  7. Next steps with an owner and a date

Design rules

  • Quiet page, one data colour, a pale tint of it for the comparison
  • One picture only, at the top, moving slowly behind the answer
  • Every chart has one job and says its unit
  • Label the values that matter and leave the rest to touch
  • Sources sit under each chart in small type
  • Limits get the same space as method
  • Below the picture, no decoration that is not data
