"Make this look better" is not a prompt

Your AI is not short of talent. It is short of a brief.

Bradley Ross. A two-minute read.

Ask an AI for a report and the thinking is often excellent. Then it arrives on a grey page, and you type the sentence everyone types: make this look better.

That sentence is a wish. The AI does not know what better means to you. It has read every book in the world and it has never met you.

Treat it as a smart intern, and hand it a brief on day one.

A brief names three things: the job, the reader and the shape. "A one-page report for my business partner, who reads on her phone and wants the answer first." Now the AI has something to design for.

I wrote the rest of my brief down, with templates, voices and photo effects, and made it free. It is called Artifact Studio. It gets you most of the way to a solid draft. The judgement stays with you.

What to check in this preview

  • Does the title make a claim someone could disagree with?
  • Does the first paragraph start with the reader, not with you?
  • Is there one line worth quoting?
  • Does the last paragraph say what to do next?

Ask for it like this

Write a short blog post from my notes in my voice: a title that makes a claim, about two hundred words, one line worth quoting. Show it as a finished page with my photo at the top.
