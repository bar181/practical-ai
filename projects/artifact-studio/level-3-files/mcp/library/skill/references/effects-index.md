# Effects index

Every effect in Artifact Studio, grouped by what the page has to do. Read this when you choose the
moving parts of a page. Each row names the effect, where it is shown working, and the words that
ask for it. Use one or two effects per page, three at most.

The words come from Tone Studio and the sections from Web Sections. This index is only about how
the page looks and moves.

## Choose by the job

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Reveal one picture as a moment | **The clip:** the photo opens from a small rounded window to full width | showcase, Cinematic effects | "Open the main photo from the centre as I scroll" |
| Give a photo depth | **Parallax window** | showcase, Photo effects | "Put my photo in a parallax window" |

## Rules for every effect

- The page is complete at rest. With scripts off or motion reduced, every effect shows its finished
  state.
- Movement follows the reader's scrolling or answers a tap. Nothing loops forever except a very slow
  photo drift.
- One grade per page. One opening per page. One stack per page.
- Effects that need a library (smooth scrolling, physics, 3D) are for built sites. See
  [Site Studio](https://github.com/bar181/practical-ai/tree/main/projects/site-studio), which runs the same cinematic effects on GSAP and Lenis.

## Effects by voice

A voice's default effects, when the person has not chosen. The voice itself is described in Tone
Studio.

| Voice | Effects that fit |
| --- | --- |
| Keynote | The opening, the statement, big numbers |
| Storyteller | Pan and zoom, the grade, the clip, end credits |
| Personal story | The grade, pan and zoom, flip postcard |
| Teacher | Scrollytelling, the timeline, before and after |
| Practical instructor | The timeline, progress ring, old way and new way |
| Analyst | Bar chart, chart from the photo, big numbers |
| Executive brief | None. A brief does not move |
