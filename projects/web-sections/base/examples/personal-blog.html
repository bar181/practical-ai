Ten minutes a day beat two hours on Saturday

For six weeks I practised for ten minutes every morning instead of two hours at the weekend. I learned more, and I did not dread it.

Your picture: the article

The Saturday plan looked sensible. Two hours, coffee, no interruptions. In practice I spent the first half hour remembering where I had stopped, and the last half hour tired.

What changed

Ten minutes is short enough that I never needed to feel ready. I stopped mid-sentence on purpose, so the next morning started with something easy to finish.

Stop while it is still going well. Tomorrow starts easier.

I will try the same thing with drawing next month, and write up how it goes.
