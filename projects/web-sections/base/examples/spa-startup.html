Three treatments, done slowly

  • 1

    Massage

    Sixty minutes, full body or back and shoulders. You choose the pressure.

  • 2

    Facial

    Forty-five minutes of cleansing, a mask and a scalp massage while it sets.

  • 3

    The long afternoon

    Both treatments, then tea in the garden room. Two and a half hours.

How a visit works

  1. Book online

    Pick a treatment and a time. It takes about two minutes.

  2. Arrive ten minutes early

    We ask three short health questions and show you to your room.

  3. Stay as long as you like

    The garden room is yours after your treatment, with tea.
