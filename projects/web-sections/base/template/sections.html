The section library

Every section a website needs, from the header and hero to the blog and footer. Each says when to use it. Copy the ones you need, in order, then set the fonts and colours in one place. All words and pictures here are invented placeholders.

Fonts

Two tokens set every font: --display for headings and --text for the rest. Both are system fonts, so the page needs no network. This is the scale every section uses.

h1, heroHeading one
h2, sectionHeading two
h3, cardHeading three
body, 17pxBody text sits at 60 to 75 characters a line, so it reads comfortably on a laptop and a phone.
small, notesSmall text for dates, captions and the legal line.

Three pairs to start from

Paste one pair over the two font lines in :root.

  • Classic, this page

    Serif headings

    Sans-serif text. Warm and trusted: shops, cafés, services.

    --display:"Iowan Old Style","Palatino Linotype",Palatino,Georgia,serif; --text:system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif;
  • Modern

    Sans everywhere

    Clean and quiet: studios, apps, galleries.

    --display:"Avenir Next","Segoe UI",system-ui,sans-serif; --text:system-ui,-apple-system,"Segoe UI",Roboto,sans-serif;
  • Editorial

    Serif everywhere

    Made for long reading: blogs, essays, newsletters.

    --display:Charter,"Bitstream Charter","Sitka Text",Cambria,serif; --text:Charter,"Bitstream Charter","Sitka Text",Cambria,serif;

Colours

Six tokens colour the whole page. Keep the balance near 60-30-10: about 60 parts calm background, 30 parts panels and text, 10 parts the one accent, kept for what to act on.

60 background30 panels and text10

Check a finished page with workshop/tools/check_colour_balance.py in the Practical AI repository.

  • --bg#fbfaf7

    The 60. Page background.

  • --panel#f1eee8

    The 30, with the text. Every other section.

  • --ink#1c2321

    Main text, 15.3 to 1 on --bg.

  • --muted#5b6461

    Secondary text, 5.8 to 1 on --bg.

  • --accent#1f6f5c

    The 10. Buttons and links. White on it is 6.0 to 1.

  • --line#dcd7ce

    Borders and rules.

Page assemblies

The sections above, in the order that works for common pages. Start here, then add or remove one section at a time.

  • Small business home page

    Site header, hero (photo or split), about, gallery, contact with hours, footer.

  • Landing page for one offer

    Site header, hero (centred or split), features, steps, quote if real, pricing, questions, call to action, footer.

  • Blog or content site

    Site header, hero (type), blog list, article, call to action, footer.

  • Portfolio

    Site header, hero (mosaic), gallery grid, about, contact, footer.

  • Event

    Site header, hero (video or photo), steps as the programme, pricing as tickets, questions, contact, footer.

  • Social post formats

    Five picture shapes for posts, stories and link previews, in the social folder, and post previews.
