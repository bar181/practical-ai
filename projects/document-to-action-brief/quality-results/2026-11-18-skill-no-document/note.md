# Note on friction

Written on 18 November 2026 by a fresh Claude session playing a chatbot with SKILL.md installed as a skill, sent no document, stepping out of role after its reply. One run, by an AI; no person has tried this. The reply beside this note is unedited.

Easy case: the skill's Start section gives the exact reply for a missing document, so I copied it word for word. One small catch: the user already said "our supplier sent" it, so we are the customer. Asking "who you are in it" again feels a little redundant. The skill says to ask nothing else, though, so I did not change the script. A possible fix: let the bot drop the optional bullet, or confirm the role in one line, when the message already makes it clear.
