# Note on friction with the skill

Written by the agent that played the chatbot, stepping out of role, on 17 November 2026. One run, by an AI; no person has tried this.

The skill fit a tender well, and the **Can we bid** row gave a clear shape. The friction was in five places. First, the biggest finding was outside the skill's rules: the tender calls 14 March, 11 April and 9 May 2027 "Saturdays", but a calendar check shows all three are Sundays. The rules say nothing may come from outside the document, yet the skill allows "worked out" date sums, so I flagged it as Q1 and marked it **worked out**. The skill should say whether checking a stated weekday against its date is allowed. I think it should be, because a caterer staffing the wrong day is the costliest miss here. Second, the reader table has no row for a business partner (a peer who decides with you), so I used the default shape and said so; a "partner or co-owner" row, or a line saying peers get the default, would remove the guess. Third, **In short** allows only two sentences but must hold a count, the earliest date and every penalty, which made a long first sentence; splitting "to bid" from "if you win" obligations helped, but the skill does not mention that split, and tenders always have it. Fourth, the checklist in **Can we bid** and the "must include" obligation repeat each other; I pointed O4 at the checklist to avoid listing the five items twice, but the skill does not say which should hold them. Fifth, the "Penalty:"/"Cost:" labels assume the reader pays: here the 50% cancellation rule is a cost to the Trust and a loss to the bidder, so the label reads oddly whichever I choose. My own check found three quotes I had capitalised ("Local", "Refreshments") that did not match the page, which shows the step 8 self-check is worth keeping.
