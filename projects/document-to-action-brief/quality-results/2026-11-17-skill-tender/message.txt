We run a small catering business and want to bid. Brief my business partner on this request.

HARROWGATE VALLEY COMMUNITY TRUST

Request for quotes: catering for the spring volunteer days

Issued: 10 November 2026

1. What we need

Lunch and refreshments for up to 120 volunteers on each of three Saturdays: 14 March, 11 April and 9 May 2027. Food must be served at the Valley Hall between 12:00 and 13:30. At least one third of the dishes must be vegetarian.

2. Who may quote

We welcome quotes from local suppliers. Suppliers must hold a current food hygiene rating of 4 or above and public liability insurance of at least $2 million.

3. Questions

Send any questions by email to procurement@harrowgatetrust.example by 24 November 2026. We will share all answers with every supplier who has asked a question.

4. What to send

Your quote must include: a price per head, a sample menu for one day, a copy of your hygiene rating, a copy of your insurance certificate, and two references from customers in the last two years.

5. Deadline

Quotes must reach procurement@harrowgatetrust.example by 12:00 noon on 4 December 2026. Late quotes will not be considered. We expect to tell suppliers the outcome in January 2027.

6. Payment

We pay within 30 days of receiving a correct invoice after each event. The Trust may cancel a day with 14 days' notice; a day cancelled with less notice will be paid at 50% of the quoted price.

Thank you for your interest.

Procurement team, Harrowgate Valley Community Trust
