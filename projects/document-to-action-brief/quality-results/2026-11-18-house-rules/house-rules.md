# House rules for Document to action brief

## Who we are
- Our business: Tallowmere Café, a 30-seat café with a small catering side
- In most documents we are: the customer
- The brief is usually for: my manager, Priya

## Documents we get
- Supplier notices and changes of terms
- Bills, tax and official letters

## Who answers what
| Kind of question | Who to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Prices, terms, delivery | our account manager at the supplier |
| Whether a term is fair or binding | our lawyer |
| Tax, payment, penalties | our bookkeeper, Sam |
| Anything else | the person who sent it |

## Where actions go
- Deadlines: the shared calendar called Café dates
- Actions: our task list called Café to-do

## Our own rules
- Flag any penalty over $30 at the very top, in bold.
- Any change to payment terms goes to our bookkeeper before anyone signs.
