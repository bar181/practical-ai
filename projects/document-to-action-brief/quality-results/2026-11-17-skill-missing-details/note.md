# Note on friction with the skill

Written by Claude (Opus 5.5) after playing the chatbot on 2026-11-17, out of role. Not tried by a person.

The skill mostly carried this notice well, but four points needed a guess. First, the boss version of **In short** says "three lines: what it is, what we must do by when, and the one question", while the general rule says In short must name "every penalty" in "two sentences at most". The two clash. I used three lines and pushed the penalty and the cost into line 2, which made that line long. Second, **What changes** asks for old and new "only where the document states both". This notice states neither (the schedule is missing), and the skill does not say whether to drop the section or keep it with a "not stated" line. I kept it and said so. Third, the user said the letter "came with our delivery" but gave no day. The skill forbids filling in a start date, so I marked the 14-day limit as cannot be worked out, but a real user might think "with our delivery" was enough and find this pedantic. Fourth, the template's "Check it against the page" asks for the first and last few words. In a letter those span line breaks (letterhead, sign-off), so a Ctrl+F on the joined words will fail; the skill could say to quote the first and last line separately. Smaller points: the skill calls the action rows "O1" but gives no rule for numbering a **Penalty:** row (I gave it its own number, O2); "Where" with no numbering left me inventing "body paragraph 1" after the greeting, which is clear but is my own scheme; and saying "the letter requires nothing unless we want to discuss" in the boss summary sits close to the "do not recommend" line, though I think it stays a reading of the text.
