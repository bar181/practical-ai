# Action brief: Kestrel Fold change of terms

**3 things to do. Earliest date: 20 November 2026 (return the signed acceptance form).**
**Costs and penalties: $18 delivery charge on orders under $200 before tax. 1.5% surcharge on card payments over $1,000. $35 admin fee per invoice unpaid after its due date, and orders may be held. Late orders move to the next delivery day.**

## What changes

All changes "take effect on 1 December 2026" (opening paragraph).

| Item | Old → new | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Order cut-off | Old: not stated. New: 2:00 pm, two working days before delivery | "orders must be placed by 2:00 pm two working days before your delivery day" (clause 1) |

**Deadline to accept:** 20 November 2026. "Please sign the acceptance form below and return it to accounts@kestrelfold.example by 20 November 2026." (clause 5)
