# Friction note

The prompt was clear and easy to follow. I still had to guess in five places. First, "things we must do": the form return is worded "Please sign", which is a request, not a "must". I counted it as one of three actions anyway and put the consequence of not signing under Open questions. Minimum order I treated as a cost, not an action. Second, "every penalty or cost" in two lines: five items fit, but only as a terse list, and I left out "Our prices are not changing". Third, "What changes" asks for old and new "where the document states both". It was unclear whether rows with only a new value belonged there. I included them with "Not stated" because leaving them out hid real changes. Fourth, the prompt names four tables but "What changes" also became a table, so the brief has five. On a phone, the four-column tables with long quotes may scroll sideways, and "one page" is doubtful at this length. Fifth, "where" for text outside numbered clauses (the date, the account number, the form) had no rule, so I invented labels: "Letter header", "Opening paragraph" and "Acceptance form". The line "two lines" also did not say whether bold or a heading was wanted; I used two bold lines.
