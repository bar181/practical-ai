Who builds it

Bradley Ross founded WiseGeo in 2016. It started with data science: targeted marketing tools and customer data platforms. Today it builds agentic software and helps companies adopt it.

Bradley researches deterministic AI, the reason he cares that agents behave the same way every time and leave a record of what they did. He also teaches it, as he did for ten terms as a Harvard CS50 Teaching Fellow.

  • Creator of AISP and BAR Observatory
  • Founding Director of the Agentics Foundation, a global community of more than 100,000 agentic developers, researchers and builders
  • Harvard CS50 Teaching Fellow, ten terms
  • Claude Certified Architect, Professional
