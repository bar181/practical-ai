Let's play Rare Find: a quiz where the correct answer nobody
else thinks of scores the most.

Category: Name a fictional weapon
(Replace it with your own, or write "surprise us" for five
family-friendly prompts on general knowledge and pop culture.)

1. Research each prompt, with web search if you can. List:
   - the 10 most popular correct answers, ranked
   - 5 rare but clearly correct answers
   - about 30 more correct answers
   - other names and spellings for each answer
   Popularity is your best estimate. Say so on the page.

2. Build ONE self-contained HTML page I can play here:
   - a 30-second ring timer per prompt (15, 30 or 45 to
     choose at the start)
   - one answer per prompt; Enter submits
   - scoring: most popular 25; 2nd and 3rd 35; 4th to 10th 50;
     any other correct answer 75; a rare one 100; wrong or
     out of time 0
   - forgive spelling: ignore case, accents, punctuation,
     spaces, hyphens and a leading "the"; allow 1 typo for
     5 to 8 letters and 2 for 9 or more; short answers and
     numbers must match exactly. Always show "counted as ..."
   - an answer not on the list scores 0, with a button that
     copies it so I can ask you to check it
   - after each answer, a reveal board: the top 10, the rare
     ones, and where mine sat
   - a final score and Play again
   - no outside images, fonts or links

3. No quotes or lyrics, no pictures of characters or logos,
   nothing on politics or religion.
