The points, one picture

How Claude is used, and where it is not

Your answerMatched against the word list first: spelling, plurals, other names, sequels.
On the listScored by the list alone. No AI involved. Instant.
Not on the list"Not on our list, 0" and a We allow it button.
We allow itClaude is asked: is it real, is it right, is it common or rare? Your text goes as data, never as instructions.
The verdictRight and common 80, right and rare 100, wrong 0. No Claude: the room decides.

Claude also writes the fresh last prompt, new categories on request, and the AI co-workers' trash talk. The co-workers' answers come from the same list by the same rules, so their scores never depend on the AI.

What is built, by level

Level 1: play

  • Solo, 5 prompts, 15/30/45 s ring timer
  • 16 prompts, no repeats game to game
  • Forgiving spelling, with 1,145 real words blocked from counting as slips
  • Reveal board, Gem banner, confetti (off with reduced motion)

Level 2: the office ladder

  • Four AI co-workers, each with a skill, a think time and a voice
  • Beat one to face the next
  • Progress and best scores saved

Level 3: Claude joins in

  • The Claude answer check
  • Trash talk after your answer, about the answer only
  • New categories and a fresh last prompt
  • Play together (beta): a room code, one prompt for everyone
