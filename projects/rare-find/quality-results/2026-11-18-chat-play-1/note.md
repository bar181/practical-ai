# Note on friction

Written by the same session after the game, stepping out of role. One run, by an AI; not ChatGPT itself.

Biggest guess: step 6 ends with a bare "Next?", but "move on when the player answers anything, including the next answer itself" assumes the player has seen the next prompt. They had not, so "Brocolli" arrived blind and I had to decide whether to score it. Showing the next prompt with "Next?" would fix this. Also unclear: whether an instruction-like answer is a Miss (🟥) or Not on our list (⬛), and its tier letter; whether a second opinion covers it or only unlisted answers; and what to do with "next" while the current prompt is still open. The skill does not say how to present the top ten when a Gem sits outside it. On a phone, a ten-row list after every prompt is long, and the challenge link appears twice in the end screen, which crowds a small display.
