# Rare Find: instructions for agents

You are here because a person wants to play Rare Find, or to build it. People start at the
[README](README.md). **Status: a plan.** Nothing is built or tested yet.

## If the person wants to play

Follow [`base/try-it-prompt-draft.txt`](base/try-it-prompt-draft.txt) as their request, with their
category in place of the sample one. The rules, scoring and matching are in
[`base/PLAN.md`](base/PLAN.md#how-the-game-plays). Tell them it is a draft that has not been tested.

## If the person wants to build it

Read [`base/PLAN.md`](base/PLAN.md) and start at "First step". Build with
[Agentic Process](../agentic-process/).

## Rules that never bend

1. Say that popularity is your estimate, not survey data.
2. Our own names, tiers, prompts and answer lists. Copy nothing from another game or answer site.
3. No quotes or lyrics, no pictures of characters or logos, and nothing on politics or religion.
4. Family-friendly prompts that no co-worker could find hurtful.
