2. Background

  1. Six weekly lessons, live on Zoom, Thursdays at 7pm Eastern, 90 minutes each. At most 20 people per group.
  2. You build a cash-flow sheet, an invoice tracker and a price calculator with your own numbers. Excel or Google Sheets both work.
  3. The teacher, Priya Shah, has been a bookkeeper for 12 years. Her reason, in her words:
    “In my first year as a bookkeeper, a florist brought me a shoebox of receipts on 29 April, two days before her tax deadline. … She was not bad with money. Nobody had ever shown her a spreadsheet slowly.”
  4. Four groups, 71 people in total, have taken the course since March 2025. In a survey afterwards, 52 answered. Of those 52, 44 said they now update their own numbers every week, and 39 said they had stopped sending their receipts to someone else to sort.
