# Source content for the seven tone examples

**Invented.** The course, the teacher, the student and every number are made up for these
examples. Each tone example uses only the facts below. Nothing else may be added.

## The facts

- **What:** "Spreadsheets Without Fear", an online course for small business owners who avoid
  spreadsheets.
- **Format:** six weekly lessons, live on Zoom, Thursdays at 7pm Eastern, 90 minutes each.
- **Starts:** Thursday 14 January 2027.
- **Price:** $249. $199 for anyone who registers before 31 December 2026.
- **Size:** at most 20 people per group.
- **What you build:** a cash-flow sheet, an invoice tracker and a price calculator, using your own
  numbers. Excel or Google Sheets both work.
- **If you miss a lesson:** each recording stays available for 30 days.
- **The teacher:** Priya Shah, a bookkeeper for 12 years.
- **Past groups:** four groups, 71 people in total, since March 2025.
- **What past students reported** (a survey after the course; 52 of the 71 answered): 44 of the 52
  said they now update their own numbers every week; 39 of the 52 said they had stopped sending
  their receipts to someone else to sort.
- **Why Priya started it, in her words:** "In my first year as a bookkeeper, a florist brought me a
  shoebox of receipts on 29 April, two days before her tax deadline. She cried at my desk. She was
  not bad with money. Nobody had ever shown her a spreadsheet slowly."
- **One past student, with permission, in her words:** "I used to dread the end of the month. Now
  it takes me twenty minutes on a Sunday." (Marta, who runs a two-person cleaning company.)
