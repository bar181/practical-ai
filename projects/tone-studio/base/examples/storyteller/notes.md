## How the order follows the tone

Both follow the storyteller order: the hook (29 April, a shoebox, a bookkeeper's desk), the reader
who wants something, the problem at three depths, the guide with empathy then proof, a three-step
plan, one call to action, failure beside success, and an ending that returns to the shoebox; the
course is not named before the fourth beat.
