## How the order follows the tone

Both run the visionary order: the world as it is in one painful number ("Two days."), the catch in
Priya's words, the idea in a few words (the course name), proof as big numbers (44 of 52, 39 of 52),
the next step (date, price, register), then a closing line short enough to repeat.
