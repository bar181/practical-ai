# Brand tone

**Example below is invented.** Replace every line with your own.

**Who we are, in one line:** [A neighbourhood bakery that remembers your order.]

**Order we use:** [Say the news first. Then why it matters to the reader. Then what to do.]

**Sentences:** [Short. First person plural ("we"). Contractions. No exclamation marks.]

**Words we use:** [regulars, bake, fresh this morning]

**Words we avoid, and what we say instead:**
- [valued customers] → [regulars]
- [we are pleased to announce] → [some news]

**Always:** [Sign off with the shop name. Give the date and the place in full.]

**Never:** [Invent a customer quote. Promise something we have not decided.]
