# Level 2: your own tone

1. **Copy** [`brand-tone-template.md`](brand-tone-template.md) and fill it in. Keep the heading
   "Brand tone" at the top: the skill looks for it.
2. **Or reuse your voice.** If you made a voice guide with
   [Write and edit in my voice](../../write-in-my-voice/), keep it beside this file. The skill uses
   its sentence habits and sign-off inside any tone.
3. **Save the instructions** with it: as a skill in Claude, or both files in a Project, a custom GPT
   or a Gem.
4. **Use it.** Paste only your content and the reader.
