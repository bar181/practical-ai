# Voice guide

**Whose voice:** [your first name]
**Written from:** [three samples, and the date]
**Example below is invented.** Replace every line with your own.

| Trait | On voice (my words) | Off voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short sentences, one idea each | "Quick one. The new grinder arrives Thursday." | "We are pleased to advise that the grinder will be delivered on Thursday." |
| Greeting | "Hi all," | "Dear Valued Customers," |
| Sign-off | "Dana" | "Kind regards, The Management" |

**Words to avoid:** "with effect from" → "from"; "commence" → "start".
