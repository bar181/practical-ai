# The brief

**Decision:** Should Greenline keep renting its second pickup each April to November, buy a used pickup, or lease a new one?

**Recommendation:** Lease a new pickup, but first get written lease and insurance quotes and go ahead only if the full-year cost is below what you paid to rent in 2026.

**Why**
- Renting a pickup in Guelph costs about $20,860 to $27,080 for an April to November season (estimate from DOZR rates). One Ontario dealer advertises a new F-150 at $175 a week, about $9,100 a year (estimate).
- A lease leaves most of your cash free to pay down the line of credit; a used truck at the average $49,717 uses 83% of your $60,000 limit.
- A new truck should not add equipment trouble; an older truck you own may.

**SWOT**
| Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- |
| Payments about $9,100 a year against $20,860 to $27,080 of rent (estimate) | Payments all year, including March, when cash is tight (profile) |
| Stays well under the $60,000 limit (option, profile) | You own nothing at the end of the term (option) |
| Same number of trucks, so no third trailer driver needed (profile) | Kilometre cap: 16,000 a year, $0.16 per km over (S4) |
| **Opportunities** | **Threats** |
| A truck all year could carry more snow-clearing work (assumed) | The $175 ad may be a loan, with no term, rate or down payment shown (S4) |
| Lease payment is well below rental rates for any size of pickup (S1, S4, estimate) | Used pickup prices are falling (down 1.1%, S3), so buying gets cheaper |
| | Insurance for a leased truck is not found and could close the gap (not found) |

**Risks and how to reduce them**
1. The real lease costs more than the ad. Get two or three written quotes with term, rate, down payment and total cost.
2. A payment lands in March when cash is tight. Ask for a start date and set aside two payments (about $1,520, estimate) before March.
3. You drive more than 16,000 km a year. Check your 2026 rental records and buy extra kilometres up front if needed.

**Next steps (next 30 days)**
1. Add up your 2026 rental invoices: rent, fuel, insurance, kilometres.
2. Ask two or three dealers for written lease quotes on a pickup rated to tow your trailer, 48 months or less.
3. Ask your insurance broker for a year-round quote and your accountant how a lease, a rental and a purchase are each treated for tax.

**What would change this recommendation**
- Lease plus insurance for a full year costs as much as your 2026 rent: keep renting.
- The $175 ad turns out to be a loan payment and no lease comes close: keep renting.
- You count a lease as debt against goal 3: keep renting.
- You look three or more years out: buying used may win.

**Assumptions**
- The rental season runs 1 April to 30 November.
- The $60,000 limit is total cost; the March and April limit is a worry, not a rule.
- A used truck brings more breakdowns than a new or rented one.
- Your rental cost is close to the Guelph rates found.
