# Calm Reply: a short, calm answer to an angry customer email

```
  YOU PASTE                                   YOU GET BACK
  a long, upset customer email                WHAT THEY WANT: one line
  "...It took TWELVE days... wrong     --->   REPLY DRAFT: calm, 120 words or fewer,
   colour... nobody answered... I             with [needs: ...] blanks for your facts
   will be leaving a review..."               NOTE: what to fill in, what to check
                                                         |
                                              you fill the blanks, edit, send it yourself
```

**Paste in the angry email. Get back a short, calm reply that never promises anything you have not decided.**

For a shop owner who already uses ChatGPT. You paste one upset customer email; you get one line
on what the customer actually wants, a calm reply draft, and a short note listing the blanks only
you can fill. Nothing is sent: you edit and send it yourself.

## Try it in five minutes

1. Open ChatGPT and start a new chat. Open [`base/AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md`](base/AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md),
   copy all of it, paste it into the chat and send it. Then paste this sample email and send it:

```
Subject: VERY disappointed - order #4471

Hi,

I am honestly so frustrated I don't even know where to start. I ordered the sage green linen
tablecloth from your shop on the 2nd of this month because it was supposed to be a birthday
present for my mother. Your website said 3 to 5 days. It took TWELVE days. The birthday was on
Saturday and I had nothing to give her, which was embarrassing in front of the whole family.

And then when it finally arrived, it is not even sage green. It's a kind of grey-blue. I double
checked my order confirmation and it clearly says sage green. So not only was it late, it's the
wrong item.

I emailed you twice last week (on Tuesday and again on Thursday) and nobody answered. Not even an
automatic reply. Is anyone actually running this business? I have bought from you three times
before and always recommended you to friends, but I won't be doing that anymore.

I want a full refund, including the shipping I paid for, and I want to know what you are going to
do about the wrong colour, because I am not paying to send it back. If I don't hear back by
Friday I will be leaving a review on Google telling people exactly what happened.

Dana Whitfield
```

2. Compare what you get with the worked example:
   [`base/examples/example-1-email-only.md`](base/examples/example-1-email-only.md).
3. Every day: keep using that chat. Paste each new customer email. When you already know a fact
   or have made a decision, add lines starting `What I know:` under the email, as in
   [`base/examples/example-2-email-with-facts.md`](base/examples/example-2-email-with-facts.md).
   Fill every `[needs: ...]` blank, read it once more, then send it from your own email.

**If you are an AI agent:** follow [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).

## What it does and what stays with you

| The AI does | You do |
| --- | --- |
| Reads the email, says what the customer wants, drafts a calm reply, marks every missing fact as a blank | Fill the blanks, make every decision (refunds, replacements, dates), edit, and send |

**Think of it as** a calm friend who reads the angry letter first and hands you a short, polite
answer, with blanks where only you know the facts.

**North Star:** a shop owner who is not technical can answer an upset customer email with a
short, calm reply in five minutes, without promising anything they have not decided.
