# Example 2: the email plus what the owner knows

**What this is:** a reference, hand-written by the building agent by following
[`AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md`](../AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md) step by step. It is not a ChatGPT output. Use it to
judge what your assistant gives you.

**Kind of request:** the owner pastes the customer email and adds `What I know:` lines, so the
draft uses those facts and promises nothing else.

## What the owner pasted

```
Subject: Where is my order???

Hello,

I ordered two candles (the cedar and the fig) on November 3rd, order 5120. It is now the 15th and
nothing has come. The tracking link you sent has said "in transit" for a week. I needed these for
a dinner on the 20th and I'm starting to think they are lost.

I have to say I'm pretty annoyed. I paid for express shipping specifically so this would not
happen. I tried calling the number on your website and it went to a voicemail that was full. This
is not how I expected a small business to treat people. I'd like to know where my parcel actually
is, and if it isn't going to arrive before the 20th I want my money back, including the express
shipping, which clearly did not happen.

Marc

What I know: The courier says the parcel is stuck at their depot and they cannot give a date.
I can send a new pair of candles today by express so they arrive by the 19th, or refund
everything including shipping. His choice. I'm Priya from Northfield Candles.
```

## What a good answer looks like

```
WHAT THEY WANT: To know where his candles are, and a full refund including express shipping if they cannot arrive before the 20th.

REPLY DRAFT:
Hello Marc,

Your order 5120 has not arrived after twelve days, even though you paid for express shipping, and you need the candles for a dinner on the 20th. I am sorry, and sorry too that our voicemail was full when you called.

The courier tells us the parcel is stuck at their depot and they cannot give a date, so please do not count on it.

You can choose:
1. We send new cedar and fig candles today by express, to arrive by the 19th.
2. We refund everything, including the express shipping.

Reply with 1 or 2 and I will get it started.

Kind regards,
Priya
Northfield Candles

NOTE
Chose: Gave him your two options plainly so he can decide in one reply.
Assumed: That "today" in your note is the day you send this. If you send it later, change "today" and "by the 19th".
Missing: Nothing
Check before sharing: He could not reach you by phone because the voicemail was full. Clear it, or he may try again and fail.
```
