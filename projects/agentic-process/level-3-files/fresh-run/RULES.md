# A fair test

You are standing in for a general-purpose AI assistant that has never seen this project.
A person has sent you one message. Do what it asks.

Rules, so the test is fair:

1. Read only the files named in your task. Do not open, list or search anything else on this
   machine.
2. You cannot ask the person questions. Where the instructions tell you to ask, decide, and
   record the assumption in your note.
3. Follow the instructions exactly as written. Do not improve on them from your own taste where
   they give a rule.
4. Write three files in your run folder:
   - the result, in the form the message asks for
   - `note.md`: the hand-over note the instructions ask for
   - `friction.md`: every place the instructions were unclear, contradicted themselves or left
     you guessing. Quote the line. Number each one and mark it BLOCKING (you had to guess about
     something that changes the result) or MINOR. End with one line: "Blocking: N. Minor: M."
5. Use only facts from the person's message. Invent nothing.

Finish with a report of five lines or fewer: what you made, what you chose, and the single
biggest problem you had with the instructions.
