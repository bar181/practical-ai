# Friction: where the instructions left me guessing

Run: supplier-notice. Instructions: COACH.md, read with RULES.md.

1. **BLOCKING. The person is not the user.**
   Quote: "Find one user and one job" and the table "Three kinds of person".
   The person builds with a coding agent and keeps a git repository (row 2). The people who will use the tool are students with any chatbot (row 1). The table treats the person and the user as one. Row 1 says "The first version is a prompt they can paste. No code". Row 2 says "Steps 5 and 6 produce the folder and the front page. Step 8 runs the checks". I chose the student as the one user and combined both rows: a paste-in prompt, kept in a repository folder. Either row alone gives a different result.

2. **BLOCKING. The step 2 gate runs backwards, and cannot be answered without a search.**
   Quote: "Go on only when the answer is yes." against the step 2 gate: "Does something existing do the whole job?" If yes, stop.
   At step 2 a yes means stop, so the general rule is wrong for this step. Worse, with no search the answer is unknown. "If you cannot, say so and list what the person should look up" does not say whether the coach then stops or goes on. I went on with an assumed no. If the true answer is yes, the whole build is the wrong result and it should have been a short guide.

3. **BLOCKING. The step 8 gate asks for a fact that does not exist yet.**
   Quote: "Did the fresh chat finish the job, and what did it trip on?" with "If you cannot ask questions at all, decide".
   I can decide a preference for the person. I cannot decide the outcome of a test nobody ran. The instructions do not say whether the coach stops at step 8 or goes on. The hand-over demands "Next three additions", which is step 9, so I went on and left the gate open. Stopping would have given a hand-over with a missing heading.

4. **BLOCKING. "Runs the checks" names checks that are never defined.**
   Quote: "Step 8 runs the checks".
   No list of checks appears anywhere. I wrote four of my own (files exist, quotes match the notice, examples say they are invented, the prompt names no product) and could run only one, because no repository path was given. Another reader would write different checks or none.

5. **BLOCKING. No test for what counts as a second job.**
   Quote: "One user and one job. If a second job appears, write it under "Next" and carry on."
   The idea has two kinds of input (supplier notice, business report) and two actions (make the list, check the list). I counted all of it as one job. Reading it as two or four jobs would have removed the check or the business report from the first version.

6. **MINOR. No way to know whether a template exists.**
   Quote: "Use the project template if they have it".
   The person did not say. I assumed none and proposed a layout.

7. **MINOR. "Fix" has two meanings.**
   Quote: "Propose a folder layout and fix it".
   It can mean settle it or repair it. I read it as settle it.

8. **MINOR. The hand-over list has no place for the folder and front page.**
   Quote: "Steps 5 and 6 produce the folder and the front page" against "What you hand over at the end", which lists the plan, the first version and a note.
   I put the layout in step 5 and the front page inside the first version.

9. **MINOR. "On one page" and "The steps" are both loose.**
   Quote: "The plan, on one page, with these headings: ... The steps."
   A chat has no page size, and ten headings press against it. "The steps" could mean the user's steps from step 6 or the coach's nine steps. I took the user's steps and held the plan to about 330 words by pointing to the layout and the pass list.

10. **MINOR. The step 7 gate assumes a reviewer who is not the coach.**
    Quote: "If nobody can, review it yourself as a stranger and list three weak points" against "Can a reviewer explain it back?"
    When the coach is the reviewer, the coach answers its own gate. I wrote the explain-back and said no person had reviewed it.

11. **MINOR. The step 9 gate leads nowhere.**
    Quote: "Is the first version in use?" with "Go on only when the answer is yes."
    Nothing follows step 9. It is not said whether the three additions are listed when the answer is no. I listed them and answered no.

12. **MINOR. Does the gate question count toward the two questions?**
    Quote: "Ask at most two questions in a step" and "End every step by ... asking the gate question."
    Step 1 needs the user, the job, what is out and "what would make people stop and look", plus the gate. I counted the gate separately.

13. **MINOR. Step 3 with no search leaves almost nothing to list.**
    Quote: "list paid options with what each covers and how it charges" against "Never invent a price, a product or a fact."
    It is not said whether the coach may name products it knows of but has not checked. I named none and listed kinds of thing to look up.

14. **MINOR. "Limit" is not defined.**
    Quote: "List every dependency of the first version with its price and its limit".
    It could be a usage cap, a size cap or what the thing cannot do. I took it as a usage or size cap.

15. **MINOR. "Decided" and "Assumed" overlap when the coach decides everything.**
    Quote: "decide, and list every decision under "Assumed" at the end" against the note heading "Decided".
    I put under Decided what follows from the person's message or is plain drafting, and under Assumed every question or gate I answered for the person.

16. **MINOR. "Keep it short" does not fit a session played in one go.**
    Quote: "Keep it short. A step is a few lines, not an essay."
    Nine steps with what was decided, the gate, the look-up lists and the full hand-over make a long document. It is not said whether the step-by-step record belongs in the hand-over at all. I kept it, short, before the three hand-over items.

17. **MINOR. The two instruction files disagree on invented data.**
    Quote: RULES.md "Use only facts from the person's message. Invent nothing." against COACH.md "Examples use invented data and say so."
    A worked example needs a document, and the person gave none. I wrote an invented notice and labelled it invented.

Blocking: 5. Minor: 12.
