2. Open ChatGPT and start a new chat. Type nothing else in it.
3. Paste the whole of the instructions and send. A good first reply is one line asking you to

4. Paste the five invented test notes from the worked example and send.
5. Check the reply against the six checks in the plan under "The test". All six must be yes.
6. Then do it once more in another new chat with your real notes from last week. That run is
