# Friction

Places where the instructions (COACH.md, and once RULES.md) were unclear, contradicted themselves or left me guessing.

1. **BLOCKING.** "A job is one kind of input turned into one result for one user. ... A second result is a second job."
   The idea is "turn a notice into key facts, obligations, dates and questions, and then check each item against the page". Nothing says whether extract-then-check is one result or two. I treated it as one result (a list whose items carry their quote and location) and left the check to the student. The other reading gives a two-pass tool, or pushes the check the person asked for into "Next".

2. **BLOCKING.** "Steps 5 and 6 produce the folder and the front page." and "every file the plan names exists"
   The coach is pasted into a chat and may have no access to the repository. "What you hand over at the end" lists a plan, a first version and a note, with no folder. Nothing says whether "produce the folder" means create files or describe them, or what check 1 should report when the coach cannot see the repository. I wrote the six files out as text and reported check 1 as not passed yet.

3. **BLOCKING.** "Does the first version cost nothing, or an amount you accept?" with "You may not decide a fact about the world: a price" and "go on as if nothing was found"
   With no search, every price is "not checked". The gate then asks for a yes that rests on a price or on the person's acceptance, and I may decide neither. "Go on as if nothing was found" does not say what the cost gate's answer is. I answered yes on the ground that nothing new is bought, which is a guess.

4. **MINOR.** "The assistant they already use counts only if it does the job with no instructions written"
   Whether it does is the result of a test, which I may not state. With no questions and no search, the step 2 gate cannot be answered honestly. I went on, and put the bare test first under "Test this next". The instructions should say that this is what to do.

5. **MINOR.** "For whatever is left, list paid options with what each covers and how it charges."
   With no search and "Never invent a price, a product or a fact", the list is empty. The gate "Do you know what you would build and what you would buy?" then passes by default with "buy nothing". The step gives no rule for an empty list.

6. **MINOR.** "They build with agents and name a repository or a folder"
   The person says they keep tools in a git repository but does not name it. It is unclear whether that counts as "name". The person also fits row 2 as the builder while their users fit row 1 ("a prompt they can paste. No code"). The table gives no rule for a builder of one kind with users of another. I used row 2 for the folder and row 1 for what the student touches.

7. **MINOR.** "Step 8 runs the checks in the template's gate file. With none, three checks" against the step 8 row, "tell the person how to test it in a fresh chat"
   For the second kind of person it is unclear whether the three checks replace the fresh-chat test or add to it. Row 3 says "Add: ... the fresh-session test in step 8", which suggests row 2 does not have it, while the steps table gives it to everyone. I did both.

8. **MINOR.** "Write one test: a specific input and what a good result looks like" (step 1), "one worked example" (steps 6 and 8) and "a fresh chat that has only those instructions"
   Nothing says whether the test input and the worked example may be the same document, or whether the worked example belongs inside the instructions. If it sits inside the prompt and is also the test input, the fresh-chat test is spoiled. I kept the example out of the prompt and used one notice for both.

9. **MINOR.** "Ask who will read the plan. If nobody can, review it yourself as a stranger"
   In a session with no questions I cannot know whether anybody can. Whether a reader exists is a fact, not a choice. The gate "Can a reviewer explain it back?" is then answered by the coach about its own plan, which proves little.

10. **MINOR.** "End every step by writing down what was decided, in two or three lines, and asking the gate question." with "What you hand over at the end"
    In a one-go session nothing says where the nine step records go, or how to record a gate answer I gave for the person. "list every decision under Assumed" does not mention gates. I put a short session record ahead of the hand-over and one line about the gates under "Assumed".

11. **MINOR.** "The plan, on one page"
    A page has no size in a chat. Ten headings, a cost table and a folder layout fit on one printed page only if each heading gets two or three lines. I kept to that, by guess.

12. **MINOR.** RULES.md: "Use only facts from the person's message. Invent nothing." against COACH.md: "Examples use invented data and say so."
    The worked example needs an invented notice. I read the RULES line as covering facts about the world and the person, and the COACH line as allowing labelled example data.

Blocking: 3. Minor: 9.
