# Teach Agentic Process in 30 minutes

A lesson plan for one teacher and any number of learners. Nobody needs to code. Each learner
needs a chatbot they already use, and one job they repeat at work.

**By the end** each learner has a one-page plan for a small AI tool of their own, and knows the
one test that finds its faults.

**Think of it as** teaching someone to build a garden deck: you will not finish the deck in half
an hour, but they will leave with the drawing, the wood list and the first board down.

## Before the lesson

| Do this | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Download [`explainer.html`](explainer.html) and open it in a browser on the screen you will present from | 1 minute |
| Open [`AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md`](AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md) and have the "Try it" prompt from the [README](../README.md#try-it-in-five-minutes) ready to share | 1 minute |
| Ask each learner to bring one job they do every week that starts from a blank page | The day before |

## The lesson

| Minutes | You do | You say | Learners do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 3 | Show the first screen of the explainer | "An AI will build whatever you ask. It will also build the wrong thing three times. Today is about the order of work that stops that." | Listen |
| 3 to 7 | Scroll to the analogy and the picture | "Think of a building site with an inspector. Nothing moves to the next stage until the current one is signed off. Our sign-off is a question called a gate." | Say one job they repeat, in one sentence, to the person beside them |
| 7 to 12 | Scroll through the four stages. Stop on each | Read the gate question for each stage aloud. On Design: "The first tool here changed its folders four times. That is what this stage prevents." | Write down which stage they usually skip |
| 12 to 22 | Share the prompt. Walk the room | "Paste it in. Change the last line to your job. Answer its questions. Stop when it reaches step 5." | Run the coach on their own idea through steps 1 to 4 |
| 22 to 26 | Show the worked example: 2 faults against 6 | "You cannot see your own gaps, because you know what you meant. Step 8 hands your instructions to a stranger." | Open a new chat, paste only the plan so far, and ask: "What is unclear here?" |
| 26 to 30 | Show the three questions under "Check yourself". Open each after the room answers | "What will you do first when you are back at your desk?" | Answer aloud. Write the next step on their plan |

## What to watch for

| You see | It means | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A learner describing three jobs | The scope is too wide | "Which one would you miss most on Monday? That one. The others go under Next." |
| The coach found an existing tool | The process worked | "Good. You just saved a week. Write the guide to that tool." |
| A learner skipping the questions to get to the build | The usual habit | "The inspector does not let you choose curtains yet." |
| A chatbot that cannot open the link | Common | "Open the file, copy all of it, paste it in before your idea." |

## After the lesson

Ask each learner to finish steps 5 to 8 in their own time and to send you two things: the plan,
and what the fresh chat said was unclear. The second one tells you more than the first.

This plan was written with the project and has not yet been taught. The timings are estimates.
Change them after your first run.
