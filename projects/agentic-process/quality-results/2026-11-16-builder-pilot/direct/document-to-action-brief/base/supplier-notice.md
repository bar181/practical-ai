**QUILLMOOR PROVISIONS**
Wholesale food and dry goods

**Notice of changes to your supply terms**

To: Lantern Step Café, account 40218
Date: 2 November 2026

**1.** Thank you for ordering with Quillmoor Provisions. This notice explains changes to our
delivery schedule, ordering and payment terms. The changes take effect on 4 January 2027 (the
"effective date").

**Delivery**

**2.** From the effective date, deliveries to your area will move from Monday and Thursday to
Tuesday and Friday.

**3.** You must confirm your preferred delivery window (7 to 9 a.m. or 9 to 11 a.m.) in the
customer portal by 1 December 2026. Accounts that have not confirmed will be assigned a window.

**4.** Standing orders will carry over where possible.

**Ordering**

**5.** The minimum order for free delivery will rise from $250 to $400 before tax.

**6.** From the effective date, orders must be placed by 2 p.m. two business days before your
delivery day.

**7.** We recommend that customers review their usual quantities before the change.

**Prices**

**8.** Prices on dairy and eggs will increase by 6%. Prices on dry goods are unchanged.

**Payment**

**9.** Customers who currently pay by cheque must switch to pre-authorized debit before the
effective date. A form is enclosed.

**General**

**10.** These terms form part of your supply agreement and replace any earlier terms on the same
subjects. Credit limits, return policies and product substitutions continue as set out in our
standard terms of sale, available on request. Invoices remain due 30 days from the invoice date.
An administration fee of $25 will be added to each invoice paid by cheque after the effective
date. Questions about this notice can be directed to your account representative.

**11.** We value your business and look forward to serving you in the new year.

Quillmoor Provisions, Customer Accounts
