## Same decision, or is one materially better?

**They are the same decision:** don't launch full catering; run a capped pilot of existing baked goods and coffee, aimed partly at afternoons, keep the head baker's hours flat, check demand first, and decide later against thresholds agreed with the partner.

The difference is in scale and proportion, not direction. B's version is somewhat better as a decision under the stated uncertainty. Its first step is free (conversations), it caps spend at $3,000, runs from one shop, and has an explicit stop rule (fewer than 5 interested offices). A commits $8,500 to $15,000 across three shops over 90 days, with up to $3,000 spent on samples before demand is proven. A gates that spending too, so the gap is meaningful but not dramatic. One caution on both: their revenue maths rest on unverified per-head prices ($15 in A, $8 in B), and those prices differ by almost 2x. Neither has local price data from the bakery itself.
