# Decision brief rubric (written before any output was read), out of 100

| Item | Weight | 100 looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sourced figures | 20 | Every figure has a source and date, or is labelled estimate with working |
| Applies the person's limits and goals | 20 | Each limit tested pass/fail; each option judged against each stated goal |
| Reproducible recommendation | 15 | The recommendation follows from a stated rule a second reader could re-apply |
| Facts, estimates and assumptions kept apart | 15 | Each claim's status is visible |
| Gaps flagged with a concrete next step | 15 | A missing fact that could change the answer is named, with a cheap, specific way to settle it |
| Shareable | 15 | A brief that stands alone, readable in about two minutes, with next steps |

Check 1 (missing context) is pass/fail: one reply, asks for the decision context, waits, does not start the analysis.
