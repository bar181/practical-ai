# Student guide: dashboard

**Think of it as** the dashboard of a car: five dials you can read at a glance, not the engine manual.

## What you need

- A chat assistant with Projects, such as Claude or ChatGPT.
- Your own material for one real week. A made-up exercise teaches less.
- About twenty minutes.

## Step 1: Try the demo

Open `level-demo/index.html`. Paste a few rows from your own sheet over the sample. Nothing leaves your browser.

## Step 2: Choose your numbers

Six at most. For each, say what "good" looks like. A number nobody acts on does not belong.

## Step 3: Tidy the sheet

One row per period. One column per number. Headings in the first row. No totals mixed in with the data.

## Step 4: Set up the Project

Paste in `level-simple/project-instructions.md`. Attach or connect the sheet.

## Step 5: Ask for the dashboard

Say "build the dashboard". Check each tile against the sheet. Then ask what the numbers do not explain.

**You are done when** you looked at the page on a Monday and changed something you were going to do that week.

## Rules the assistant follows

- Every number on the page is in the sheet, and the page says which sheet and which date.
- It shows the change from the last period for every number.
- It never draws a chart with two scales.
- It says when a number is missing or looks wrong, and does not guess.
