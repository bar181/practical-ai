Behind this page

The keynote voice sells a vision by removing everything else. Inspired by Steve Jobs's product launches. The company and every figure here are invented for the demo.

How it is built

  1. The world as it is, as one painful number
  2. The consequence, said quietly
  3. A question that opens the door
  4. The answer in three words
  5. One image and the new number
  6. Before and after, side by side
  7. One more thing
  8. A closing line short enough to repeat

Design rules

  • One idea per screen. If it needs two sentences, it is two screens.
  • Black background, one typeface, white and grey only
  • Numbers are the pictures. Make them enormous.
  • The rule of three: three words, three steps, three benefits
  • One photograph, used once, full screen
  • Each screen snaps into place and its words arrive in order
