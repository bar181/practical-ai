Artifact Studio: the North Star

What this project is for, who it serves, what is in it, and how anyone can tell whether it is working.

The North Star

A person with no design training gives an assistant their own notes and one sentence, and gets back something they are proud to send, on the first or second try.

Think of it as the design brief you hand a smart intern.AI has read every book in the world and has never met you. It can build a page in seconds. It does not know what good looks like for this job, this reader or your business. The studio tells it.

The problem it solves

Your AI gives brilliant answers. Then it shows them on a grey page.

By handA deck you like takes an hour to several days: pick a theme, copy and paste, hunt for images, fix the alignment, start again.
A chatbot aloneFast, and it looks like everyone else's. Even experienced users lose hours asking for one fix after another.
With the studioThe chatbot starts from a curated template, a voice and a look. You get most of the way to a draft you can use, in minutes.

Who it is for

One library, three kinds of user. Each has a guide beside this page.

Anyone with a chatbotPaste one link or one set of instructions and say what you want. Nothing to install. This is level 1, and it is the whole product.
People who make things every weekA preferences file and a skill hold your brand, tone and rules, so a short ask comes back in your style. This is level 2.
Agents and teamsA server gives agents the library as tools, so they choose and build without a person picking templates. This is level 3.

How a request becomes a file

Four stages, always in this order. Each one narrows the next. Scroll and the diagram keeps your place.

  1. Stage 1. Three choices

    Choose the template, the voice and the look

    Start with the job of the piece: who reads it and what they should do next. That picks the template. The template suggests a voice. The voice suggests a look.

    • Name them yourself, or let the picker score every template
    • Or take the colours from a photo you attach
  2. Stage 2. Words before pixels

    Write the outline and list the gaps

    Your content is mapped onto the template's sections, in the order the voice sets. Anything missing is marked on the page as a gap. Nothing is invented to fill it.

    • No made-up numbers, names or quotes
    • Every assumption is written down
  3. Stage 3. One file

    Build it from parts that carry content

    Each section gets a part only if it needs one: big numbers for results, a timeline for a schedule, a table for a choice. The result is one file that opens anywhere.

    • Decks and social posts start from a finished starter file
    • Designed for a phone first
  4. Stage 4. Before anyone sees it

    Check it, then look once

    A short checklist, then one read on a phone. The piece comes back with a note: what was chosen, what was assumed, what is missing and what you should verify.

    • A person decides whether it is shared

For agents

The same library, as a skill and as tools. An agent follows nine steps and returns the piece with a note in this form.

Chose: proposal, practical instructor, brand spec.
Assumed: the start date is the first Monday after acceptance.
Missing: [needs: payment terms]
Check before sharing: the three option prices against the estimate.

Three rules never bend: no invented facts, a voice is a style and never an impersonation, and nothing is posted or published without a person saying so.

How we will know it is working

Four measures, in the order they should be met. None has been met yet.

  1. One other person makes something realSomeone who did not build this presents a deck or sends a report made with it, for an actual job.
  2. First or second tryThey get something they are willing to send without a third round of fixes.
  3. The sentences hold up in fresh chatsEach short ask is run in a new conversation and scored against the checklist.
  4. More than one chatbotThe same sentence gives a usable result in at least two different assistants.

What is proven and what is not

CheckedEvery tab of the studio page and every template opens in Chrome at phone and desktop width with no errors and no sideways scrolling. An automated accessibility scan found no violations. The picker gives the same answer in the page and in the server.
Not yet testedThe sentences have not been run in fresh chats and scored. It was built with one assistant and has not been run in the others. No Safari or Firefox.
EstimatesThe times on the landing page are estimates, not measurements. The template scores are the author's judgement.
A guessThe practical instructor voice is a best guess at one person's teaching style and has not been corrected by him.
