# Example: a week of social pictures

A worked example: what was asked, what came with it, what the studio chose and what came back. The business and every figure are invented.

## The request

> We are running winter service week. I need a square post and a three-slide carousel for it. Make them stand out. Our colours are navy and yellow.

## The notes that came with it

- We are a bike shop
- Winter service week is 12 to 18 January
- A full winter service is $60 that week. It is usually $85
- It covers brakes (pads checked, cables adjusted), gears (cleaned, indexed, oiled) and tyres (checked for cuts, pumped)
- People book at the counter

## What the studio picked

For "tell people something new", "people who do not know us" and "a social post", the picker chose **Square post** (strong fit, 15 of 15). Square post: a strong fit for "tell people something new", a strong fit for "people who do not know us".

## How it was made

The assistant copied the social starters from [`templates/starters/`](../../templates/starters/) and replaced the words. "Navy and yellow" matched the Bold style, so each colour was swapped for that style's, using the list under `social_styles` in the index.

## The output

- [`square.svg`](square.svg): the square post, 1080 by 1080
- [`carousel-1.svg`](carousel-1.svg): carousel slide 1, the hook
- [`carousel-2.svg`](carousel-2.svg): carousel slide 2, the three things
- [`carousel-3.svg`](carousel-3.svg): carousel slide 3, the price and the ask

## The note that came back with it

- **Chose:** the square post and the three-slide carousel you asked for, in the Bold style because you said navy and yellow.
- **Assumed:** that "book at the counter" is the only way to book.
- **Missing:** the shop's name. The square post shows a placeholder.
- **Check before sharing:** the dates and the two prices. To post, open each file in the Social tab's download, or export it as a PNG.

## What a good result must have

1. Each picture has one idea and a headline of seven words or fewer
2. The dates and both prices match the notes exactly
3. The carousel runs hook, then the three things, then the ask
4. The colours are navy and yellow, and every text can be read against its background
5. The shop's name is a placeholder, because the notes did not give one

## Try it yourself

Give an assistant the request and the notes above, with the starter attached. Score what comes back against the list above, one point for each. Record the result in [`../README.md`](../README.md).
