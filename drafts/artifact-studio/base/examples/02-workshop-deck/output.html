By the end

You will be able to check any order for allergens in under a minute.

And know what to do when you are not sure.

One promise. Ask who has packed an order this week.

Think of it as

A seat belt.

It takes a second. You do it every time. You only find out why on the one day it matters.

Give the picture before the steps.

The three steps

  1. Read the allergen line on the order label.
  2. Match it against the tray card.
  3. If they differ, or one is missing, stop and ask the shift lead.
Read each step once, slowly. The example does the explaining.
