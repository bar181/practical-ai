# Colours, from hue 320

| Use | Colour |
| --- | --- |
| Page background | `#f9f3fa` |
| Panels and cards | `#eee4f0` |
| Borders and rules | `#d9ccdb` |
| Secondary text | `#655868` |
| Main text | `#211623` |
| Buttons and links | `#793887` |
| Highlights behind text | `#f4dafa` |

## Contrast

| Pair | Ratio | Needs | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main text on the page background | 16.0 to 1 | 7 | Passes |
| Secondary text on the page background | 6.1 to 1 | 4.5 | Passes |
| Secondary text on panels | 5.4 to 1 | 4.5 | Passes |
| White text on a button | 7.7 to 1 | 4.5 | Passes |
| Links on the page background | 7.0 to 1 | 4.5 | Passes |
| Main text on a highlight | 13.5 to 1 | 7 | Passes |

## Rule

About 60 parts background, 30 parts text and structure, 10 parts accent, judged by eye and not by counting pixels. The accent is for things to act on and nothing else.
