Seven voices

One message, told seven ways. Every page below makes the same case with the same facts: a kitchen renovation company should answer each enquiry within the hour. The voice changes what is said, in what order, and what the page looks like.

The company, the people and the figures are invented for these demos.

  • The keynote

    One idea per screen. Huge type. A vision, not a report.

    Inspired by Steve Jobs

  • The storyteller

    The customer is the hero. The company is the guide.

    Inspired by Donald Miller

  • The teacher

    An everyday analogy, then an example, then the detail.

    Inspired by David Malan

  • The pitch

    First person. A feeling first, the product last.

    Inspired by Don Draper

  • The practical instructor

    The core idea, one analogy, steps for today.

    A best guess at Bradley Ross

  • The analyst

    The answer first, then three findings with evidence.

    Inspired by Barbara Minto

  • The executive brief

    A decision memo. Plain on purpose.

    The board paper convention
