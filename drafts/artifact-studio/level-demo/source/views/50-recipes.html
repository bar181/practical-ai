Four recipes

A recipe is a whole page type: what goes on it and in what order. Each example below is a finished page made from the components on the other tabs, with a made-up business as the subject.

  • One-page report

    How did it go, and what happens next. A staffing agency's monthly service report.

  • Proposal

    What you asked for, what we suggest, three prices and a schedule. A kitchen renovation.

  • Scrolling explainer

    A process taught one stage at a time, with a diagram that keeps your place.

  • Landing page

    One promise, one button. A bookkeeping catch-up service.
