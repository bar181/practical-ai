Questions and answers

Each question opens on a tap. People read only the ones they came with.

Does anything get sent without a person seeing it?

No. Every reply waits for a named member of staff to read it and press send.

What if the draft is wrong?

Edit it, or delete it and write your own. Guesses are marked so you check those first.

How long does it take to set up?

About an hour for one mailbox, using three replies you have already sent.
