Behind this page

A landing page has one job: get one kind of visitor to take one action. Everything on it either builds the case or removes a doubt. This one uses a plain confident voice and a moving ledger in place of a photograph.

Recipe: the order of the page

  1. The promise in one line, with a number in it
  2. One button, repeated at the end
  3. A picture of the result (here, a ledger being ticked off)
  4. What you get, as three things
  5. How it works, in three steps
  6. One customer's words
  7. The price, stated plainly
  8. The three questions people really ask
  9. The promise again, and the button

Components used

  • Price cards
  • Questions and answers
  • A customer quote
  • A small looping animation built from real-looking rows

Goes well with

  • Voices: the storyteller, the pitch, the practical instructor
  • Looks: two strong colours and one for the button, used nowhere else
