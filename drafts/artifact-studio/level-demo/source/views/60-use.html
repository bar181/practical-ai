How to use the studio

The studio is a menu. You point at what you want and the assistant builds it from your own content. There are three levels, and you stop at the one that is enough.

Three levels

BeginnerOne sentence and your notes.
You give
Notes or a document, and a sentence copied from this studio
You get
A good page in one reply, in the assistant's choice of colours
Time
Five minutes
AdvancedYou choose the recipe, voice, look and parts.
You give
The longer sentence, your colours and fonts on a one-page spec, and a photo if you have one
You get
A page that looks like yours and sounds the way you chose
Time
Half an hour, including one round of changes
ExpertThe skill does the whole job and checks its own work.
You give
Any content, images or video, and the artifact-studio skill installed
You get
A finished page, a list of what was assumed, and a pre-ship check
Time
An hour to set up once, then minutes per page

For people: five steps

  1. Open the Start tab and build your sentence, or copy one from under any example.
  2. Paste it into Claude or ChatGPT and attach your notes. Add a photo if the page should use one.
  3. Read what comes back on your phone first. Most people will see it there.
  4. Ask for changes by naming parts: "make the big numbers a ring", "use the teacher voice for the opening".
  5. Check every number and name against your notes before you share it.

For agents: the skill

The repository holds a skill called artifact-studio. It turns any content into a page by following the same menu, in this order: intake, recipe, voice, look, outline, parts, build, check.

  • It invents no facts. A gap in the content is marked on the page and listed for the person.
  • It uses real customer words for quotes, or leaves the quote out.
  • It builds one self-contained file that works on a phone and with motion turned off.
  • It runs the pre-ship checklist and reports the result.
  • Anything that posts, sends or publishes waits for a person.

CheckedEvery tab renders in Chrome at phone and desktop width with no script errors.
Not yet checkedThe sentences have not been run in fresh chats and scored. The results sheet for that test is in the repository.
InventedEvery company, person and figure in the examples is made up. The two photographs are real.
