# Level simple (beginner): one sentence

The smallest version you can run yourself. No accounts to connect, nothing to install.

**Think of it as** pointing at a picture on a menu.

## What you do

1. Pick the sentence that matches the page you need from [`sentences.md`](sentences.md), or build
   one on the Start tab of the [demo](../level-demo/).
2. Paste it into Claude or ChatGPT.
3. Attach or paste your notes underneath. Add a photo if the page uses one.
4. Read the result on your phone and check every number against your notes.

That is the whole level. The [student guide](../STUDENT-GUIDE.md) walks through it with a table of
what to say when something goes wrong.

## What is here

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [`sentences.md`](sentences.md) | Every sentence in the studio: 4 recipes and 7 voices at three levels each, 12 page components, 12 photo components |
| [`sentence-test.md`](sentence-test.md) | How to test whether a sentence works, and the sheet to record results |

## What to expect

- A beginner sentence leaves the colours and type to the assistant. The page will be sound and
  may look plain. Level 2 fixes that.
- The sentences have not yet been scored in fresh chats. If one fails for you, add a row to the
  results sheet. That is how they get better.

## Rules that still apply at this level

- The assistant must not add facts. If it does, tell it to remove anything not in your notes.
- You decide whether the page is sent or published.
