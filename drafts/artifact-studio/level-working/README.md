# Level working (advanced): your recipe, voice, look and parts

The common size. You make the choices the assistant made for you at the beginner level, and the
page starts to look and sound like yours.

**Think of it as** ordering off the menu with your usual changes, which the kitchen keeps on file.

## One-time setup, about 20 minutes

1. Fill in [`design-spec-template.md`](design-spec-template.md): colours, fonts, three things you
   always want and three you never want. Keep it. You attach it to every request.
2. Save the spec in a chat Project together with the four cards below, so you do not attach them
   each time.

| Card | What it settles |
| --- | --- |
| [Recipes](../../../skills/artifact-studio/references/recipes.md) | What goes on the page and in what order |
| [Voices](../../../skills/artifact-studio/references/voices.md) | How it speaks, and what comes first |
| [Looks](../../../skills/artifact-studio/references/looks.md) | Colour, type and layout, and how to take a palette from a photo |
| [Components](../../../skills/artifact-studio/references/components.md) | The parts you can ask for by name |

The cards live in the skill folder so there is one copy of each.

## For each page, about 30 minutes

1. Fill in [`content-brief-template.md`](content-brief-template.md). Five lines.
2. Choose the recipe, the voice and the look. The Mix tab of
   [`artifact-studio-advanced.html`](artifact-studio-advanced.html) builds the request for you.
3. Paste the sentence with your brief and your notes.
4. Ask for parts by name, and for changes by naming the part: "replace the bar chart with big
   numbers", "use the teacher voice for the opening only".
5. Run the [pre-ship checklist](../../../skills/artifact-studio/references/pre-ship-checklist.md).

## What is here

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [`artifact-studio-advanced.html`](artifact-studio-advanced.html) | The level 2 page: 7 voices as full pages, 9 looks, the request mixer, the cards and both templates |
| [`design-spec-template.md`](design-spec-template.md) | Your colours, fonts and rules. Fill in once |
| [`content-brief-template.md`](content-brief-template.md) | Who the page is for and what it must do. Fill in per page |

## When to move up a level

Move to the expert level when you make a page most weeks, or when more than one person needs to
produce pages that match.
