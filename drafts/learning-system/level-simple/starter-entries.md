# Starter entries

Invented examples for three audiences. Use them to see what a good row looks like, then replace
them with your own. The same columns work for all three.

## For developers

| Question | Recommended solution | Context | Keywords |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The build fails with "module not found" after pulling the main branch | Delete the installed dependencies folder and install again. The lock file changed. | Projects with a lock file | cannot find module; missing dependency |
| Tests pass on my machine and fail in the pipeline on dates | Set the time zone explicitly in the test setup | Any test that formats or compares dates | timezone; UTC; off by one day |
| The batch job gets "429 too many requests" | Send 50 at a time and wait between batches, doubling the wait after each refusal | Calls to any rate-limited service | rate limit; throttled; backoff |

Developers often add two columns: the exact error text, and the environment it happened in.

## For a business

| Question | Recommended solution | Context | Keywords |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A customer asks to change the order after sign-off | Price the change in writing on the change form. No work until it is signed. | Any job with a signed quote | variation; extra work; scope change |
| A client disputes a line on the invoice | Send the signed quote and the change record. Offer a call within one working day. | Invoices after completion | dispute; query; not agreed |
| A new starter needs access to the systems | Request it on the Monday form. Access takes two working days. | All new staff | onboarding; login; account |

Businesses often add one column: who to ask if the answer does not fit.

## For personal use

| Question | Recommended solution | Context | Keywords |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The printer shows as offline again | Turn the printer off. Unplug the router for 30 seconds. Turn the printer on last. | The home printer | printer; offline; cannot print |
| The bread came out dense | Let it rise longer. Below 20 degrees in the kitchen, add two hours. | The usual loaf | sourdough; dense; did not rise |
| A warning light came on after refuelling | Tighten the fuel cap. The light clears after two drives. | The family car | engine light; fuel cap |

For personal use, drop "Confirmed by". You are the only one confirming.
