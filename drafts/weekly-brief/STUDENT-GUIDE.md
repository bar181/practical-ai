# Student guide: weekly brief with research

**Think of it as** a chief of staff's Friday note: what changed out there, what we decided in here, and what is still open.

## What you need

- A chat assistant with Projects, such as Claude or ChatGPT.
- Your own material for one real week. A made-up exercise teaches less.
- About twenty minutes.

## Step 1: Write your topic list

Five lines at most: your industry, two competitors or peers, one regulation you watch, one thing you are curious about.

## Step 2: Set up the Project

Paste in `level-simple/project-instructions.md` and your topic list.

## Step 3: Each Friday, attach the week's inside material

Meeting notes, transcripts and any document that mattered. Leave out anything you would not want summarised.

## Step 4: Ask for the brief

Say "this week's brief". The assistant searches for outside news on your topics and reads what you attached.

## Step 5: Check three links

Open three of the outside links. If a claim is not on the page it links to, say so. That is how the brief earns trust.

**You are done when** you have acted on something from the brief that you would otherwise have missed.

## Rules the assistant follows

- Every outside claim has a link and the date it was published.
- Every inside claim names the document or meeting it came from.
- Outside and inside are kept in separate sections.
- It says "nothing new" when there is nothing new.

## Open slots in this draft

- The research behind this draft is notes only. Sources have not been checked.
- Connecting an assistant to your own accounts differs by product and has not been walked end to end.
- Nobody other than the author has used this guide.
