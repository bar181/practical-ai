# Weekly brief with research: landscape notes

**Sources not checked.** These are notes from general knowledge, written on 2026-11-15 to get the
simple level started. They are not a landscape review. The review, with dated sources and prices,
is still to do and is the next step for this tool.

## Established practice

A short recurring brief that separates what happened from what it means is standard in staff work and newsrooms. The discipline that matters is the source on every claim.

## What assistants already do

Chat assistants can search the web and read attached files, and some can run a task on a schedule. The quality of a brief depends on the topic list more than on the tool.

## The real risk

A fluent summary with a wrong or missing source. The instructions require a link and a date for every outside item and ask the reader to spot-check three each week.
