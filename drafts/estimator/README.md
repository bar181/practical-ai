# Estimator

A first-draft quote with its reasoning, built from your own price list and your own written rules.

**Think of it as:** an apprentice estimator who has memorised your price list and your rule book.
They work out the quote, show every step, and hand it to you to check. They never guess a price
and they never send anything to a customer.

**Who it is for:** any business that prices work from a rate card or a price list and a set of
habits that currently live in someone's head.

## Three worked examples

All figures are illustrative units, not market prices. No real company is described.

| Example | Kind of business | What makes it different |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Management consulting](level-simple/examples/management-consulting/) | Sells people's time | Day rates by role, a percentage for project management, contingency when scope is unclear |
| [Kitchen renovation](level-simple/examples/kitchen-renovation/) | Sells materials and labour by measure | Unit prices by area and length, flat fees, contingency that depends on the age of the house |
| [Employment services](level-simple/examples/employment-services/) | Places full-time and temporary staff | A percentage fee on salary for permanent hires, an hourly bill rate from a pay rate and a multiplier for temporary staff |
