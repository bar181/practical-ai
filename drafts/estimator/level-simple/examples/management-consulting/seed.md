# Seed: management consulting

A generic consulting practice that sells people's time. Figures are illustrative units, not market
prices.

## Price list

| Item | Unit | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead consultant | Day | 1,800 | Leads the work and the client relationship |
| Consultant | Day | 1,200 | Interviews, analysis, drafting |
| Analyst | Day | 800 | Data gathering and analysis |

## Rules

1. Every engagement starts with discovery: 3 lead consultant days, unless the brief says otherwise.
2. Each workshop takes 2 lead consultant days and 2 consultant days. That covers one day to prepare and one day to deliver, for each person.
3. A written report takes 2 lead consultant days and 3 consultant days.
4. Interview, analysis and data work are estimated in days by role from the size of the job. Show the days you chose and why.
5. Add project management at 10% of the labour subtotal.
6. If the scope is not yet fixed (for example, the number of interviews is unknown), add a contingency of 10% of the labour subtotal plus project management.
7. Round the final total to the nearest 100.
8. Travel and other expenses are charged at cost and are never included in the estimate. Say so.
9. Payment: 40% at the start, 60% on delivery of the report.
10. An estimate is valid for 30 days.
