# Level working: inbox triage and daily summary

**Status: outline.** Written from the simple level. Not yet used by anyone.

## What this level adds

A read-only connection to your mailbox and calendar, and a saved list of rules that grows from your corrections.

## What stays the same

The instructions and their rules. A person still approves anything that goes out.

## When to move up a level

When you use it every week and your corrections have stopped changing the rules.
