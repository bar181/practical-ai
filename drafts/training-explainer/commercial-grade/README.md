# Commercial grade: what a commercial build adds

This folder explains. It never holds a project.

## What changes

| Area | In this repository | In a commercial build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Likeness and voice | Your own, by your say-so | Consent, licences and expiry tracked for every presenter |
| Quality | You watch it against the brief | Subject review, caption accuracy and accessibility checks |
| Reach | One language, one course | Captions and translations, delivered through a learning platform |
| Consistency | Your visual rules on a page | A design system for lessons, applied to every video |
| Measurement | None | Completion and quiz results fed back into the next version |

## What does not change

- No invented facts.
- A person approves anything that goes out.

## Signs you have outgrown the levels here

- A presenter left and their avatar is still teaching
- Learners in another language cannot follow it
- Nobody knows which lessons people stop watching
