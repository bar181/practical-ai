# Short video guide: landscape review

What guides say about the opening seconds of a short video and about watching without sound.
Checked on 2026-11-15. The tool was drafted before this review. One check was added as a result.

## Summary

- **Everything is decided in the opening seconds.** Every guide found says the first two to three
  seconds settle whether a viewer stays. The script's first beat is already a two-second hook.
- **Plan for no sound.** Guides agree many people watch muted and that on-screen words must
  carry the opening. **They disagree widely on how many.** Figures found ranged from about four
  in ten to more than eight in ten, and none linked to a study that could be checked. No figure
  is repeated in this tool.
- **One free test appears in several guides** and is now in the skill: watch the first three
  seconds with the sound off.

## Established practice

| Practice | What the sources say | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook at once | The opening two to three seconds decide whether a viewer keeps watching. No warm-up | [Sendible](https://www.sendible.com/insights/video-hook-ideas), [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/create/short-form-video-hooks-first-3-second-patterns) |
| Do not open with a greeting, your name or your brand | Get to the point of the hook immediately | [Captions](https://captions.ai/help/guides/marketing/hook-writing.md) |
| Words on screen from the first frame | On-screen text serves people watching without sound and reinforces the line for everyone else | [Sendible](https://www.sendible.com/insights/video-hook-ideas), [tlinky](https://tlinky.com/3-second-hook/) |
| Test with the sound off | Watch only the first three seconds, muted. Can you tell what it is about and who should care? | [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/create/short-form-video-hooks), [Vivideo](https://vivideo.ai/blog/how-to-hook-viewers-short-form-video) |
| The best opening is often not the first sentence | When cutting from a longer recording, the hook may sit well into it | [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/create/short-form-video-hooks) |
| Show the value early | Do not tease for most of the video. Show, then explain | [Torro](https://torro.io/blog/the-3-second-rule-why-no-ones-watching-your-reels) |

**Read this with care.** Nearly every source here sells a video or captioning product. Their
advice agrees, which makes it agreed practice. Their statistics do not agree and are left out.

## How this tool follows it

| Practice | In this tool |
| --- | --- |
| Hook at once | The first beat is the hook, zero to two seconds, with the surprising line |
| Words on screen | Each beat has what is said and what is shown. The hook shows three to five large words |
| Write the ending first | Already in the skill. Not from these sources |
| Test with the sound off | **Added after this review.** The skill reads the on-screen words of the first three seconds alone and checks they say what the video is about |
| No greeting, name or brand first | **Added after this review**, to the same step |
