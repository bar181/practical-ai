# Script template: the four beats

Copy this table. Fill the two right-hand columns. About 50 spoken words fills 20 seconds.

| Beat | Seconds | Spoken | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0 to 2 | The surprising line | 3 to 5 words, large |
| Point | 2 to 12 | The one idea, plainly | One supporting image or phrase |
| Proof | 12 to 17 | A number, an example or a result | The number |
| Ask | 17 to 20 | One thing to do next | The action |
