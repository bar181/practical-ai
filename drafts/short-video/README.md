# Short video guide

Pick the right route by length and purpose, and finish a short video in under 30 minutes.

**Think of it as:** getting across a city. A scooter, a bus and a taxi all work, but each suits a
different trip. Most wasted effort in AI video is taking the taxi for a one-block trip.

**Who it is for:** anyone who posts, teaches or pitches. No editing skills assumed.
