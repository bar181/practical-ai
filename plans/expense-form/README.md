# Expense form creator

A folder of receipt photos becomes a filled-in expense form in your own categories.

**Think of it as:** A bookkeeper's assistant who sorts the shoebox of receipts and fills in the form, then waits for you to check it.

## What it includes

- Simple: receipt images in, a categorised form out, using a template you provide.
- Working: a connected folder, your own category list with accounting and tax codes.
- Automated: an approval step, then posting to an accounting system.
- Categories and codes come from your own list. None are assumed.
