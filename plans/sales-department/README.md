# Agentic marketing and sales department

A set of agents that cover the routine work of marketing and sales, each with a person approving.

**Think of it as:** A small office where each desk has a clear job and a manager signs off before anything leaves the building.

## What it includes

- Website chatbot: answers from your own notes and captures enquiries.
- Lead responder: a fast, qualified reply to each new enquiry.
- Outreach agent: follow-up sequences in your voice, sent on approval.
- Call agent: answers common questions and books appointments. Inbound first.
- The blueprint: how these connect to one customer record.
