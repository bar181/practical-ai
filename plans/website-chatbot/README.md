# Website chatbot

A site assistant that answers from your own notes and captures enquiries.

| Complexity | Research | Simple level | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner to advanced | Not started | Not started | None yet |

**Think of it as:** A receptionist who knows your price list and your opening hours, takes a message when unsure, and never makes a promise you did not write down.

## Overview

A site assistant that answers visitors from your own notes and captures enquiries.

## Who it is for

Any business whose website gets the same ten questions.

## What might be involved at each level

The simple level is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demo | A sample assistant answering from a synthetic set of notes. | Beginner |
| Simple | Write one page of facts: what you offer, prices you publish, hours, how to reach you, and the ten questions you hear most. Load it into a chatbot Project and test it with awkward questions before it goes near a website. | Beginner |
| Working | Embedded on the site, answers only from your notes, and collects a name and a question when it cannot answer. | Intermediate |
| Automated | Books meetings, hands a conversation to a person, and records enquiries in your customer system. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | Conversation records, consent and privacy notices, languages, and uptime monitoring. | Explained, not built here |
