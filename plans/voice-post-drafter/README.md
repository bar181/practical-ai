# Voice post drafter

A draft that sounds like you, for you to copy, edit and publish.

**Think of it as:** A ghostwriter who has read everything you posted and remembers every time you said "I would never say that". They hand you a draft. You still sign it.

## What it includes

- Voice comes from examples you wrote, not from adjectives.
- Stated preferences are read before anything is drafted.
- One idea per post. Facts come from your notes or from you.
- A person edits and publishes. Nothing posts itself.
- A daily topic scout is the automated level: topics and drafts queued for review.
