# Proposal builder

Notes from a client conversation, or a finished estimate, become a formatted proposal.

| Complexity | Research | Simple level | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner to advanced | Not started | Not started | None yet |

**Think of it as:** A document specialist who takes your scribbled notes and your price and returns something you would be glad to sign.

## Overview

Notes from a client conversation, or a finished estimate, become a formatted proposal in your own structure.

## Who it is for

Anyone who writes proposals and reuses the same sections each time.

## Scope

- **In:** the simple level described below.
- **Out:** the working level and beyond, until the simple level is in use.
- **It works when:** to be written before anything is built.

## What might be involved at each level

The simple level is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demo | A sample proposal produced from synthetic notes and an estimate. | Beginner |
| Simple | Write one proposal template with your standard sections. Give your assistant the template, your notes and the price. It fills in the proposal and lists anything it had to assume. You check it and send it. | Beginner |
| Working | Templates and past proposals in a connected folder, with terms pulled from your own standard wording. | Intermediate |
| Automated | Sent for signature, with a deposit invoice raised when it is accepted. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | Approval for discounts, clause libraries, and tracking of what was sent and signed. | Explained, not built here |
