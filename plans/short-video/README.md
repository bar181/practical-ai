# Short video guide

Pick the right route by length and purpose and finish a short video in under 30 minutes.

| Complexity | Research | Simple level | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner to intermediate | Partly: tool facts checked 2026-11-14 | [Drafted](../../drafts/short-video/) | [`drafts/short-video/`](../../drafts/short-video/) |

**Think of it as:** Getting across a city. A scooter, a bus and a taxi all work, but each suits a different trip.

## Overview

A route table that matches the kind of video you need to the cheapest way of making it, plus a script format that works for every route.

## Who it is for

Anyone who posts, teaches or pitches. No editing skills assumed.

## What might be involved at each level

The simple level is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demo | A finished script and one finished video per route. | Beginner |
| Simple | Write a four-beat script (hook, point, proof, ask) of about 50 words. Choose a route: a generated clip under 8 seconds, a free explainer made from your documents, a 20-second short with your own avatar and voice, or code-driven motion graphics. Follow that route's steps in the tool's own app, then run the checklist before posting. | Beginner |
| Working | A saved prompt set per route and a voice file, so every video sounds like you. | Intermediate |
| Automated | An agent runs the route end to end and stops at your approval. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | A content calendar, brand review, rights and consent records, and volume rendering. | Explained, not built here |
