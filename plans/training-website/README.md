# Training website

A course site with lessons, a schedule and a built-in assistant.

| Complexity | Research | Simple level | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner to advanced | Not started | Not started | None yet |

**Think of it as:** A classroom noticeboard that is always up to date: this week's lesson, what is due, where the materials are.

## Overview

A course site with lessons, a schedule and resources in one place, with a course assistant built in.

## Who it is for

Instructors and course builders.

## What might be involved at each level

The simple level is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demo | A sample course site for a synthetic three-lesson course. | Beginner |
| Simple | One page per course built as an artifact: the outline, this week's lesson, what is due, and links to materials. Update it by asking your assistant to revise the page. | Beginner |
| Working | Several pages, a schedule that updates itself, and the student assistant embedded. | Intermediate |
| Automated | Sign-in for students, progress tracking, surveys and reminders. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | Enrolment and payments, certificates, accessibility review and support. | Explained, not built here |
