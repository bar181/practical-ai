# Client onboarding kit

A welcome pack and a checklist for each new client, tailored from what you already know about them.

| Complexity | Research | Simple level | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner to intermediate | Not started | Not started | None yet |

**Think of it as:** A hotel welcome folder made for one guest: their name on the cover, their schedule inside.

## Overview

A welcome pack and a checklist for each new client, written from what you already know about them.

## Who it is for

Anyone who starts new client work more than a few times a year.

## Scope

- **In:** the simple level described below.
- **Out:** the working level and beyond, until the simple level is in use.
- **It works when:** to be written before anything is built.

## What might be involved at each level

The simple level is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demo | A sample welcome pack and checklist for a synthetic client. | Beginner |
| Simple | Write one welcome template and one checklist. Give your assistant the template and your notes from the sales conversation, and ask for a tailored pack: what happens in week one, who to contact, what you need from them. | Beginner |
| Working | Filled in from your customer records and the signed proposal. | Intermediate |
| Automated | The kickoff is scheduled and the tasks are created when a deal is marked won. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | Templates by service line, sign-off steps and a handover record. | Explained, not built here |
