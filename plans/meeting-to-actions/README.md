# Meeting to actions

A transcript becomes decisions, owners and dates.

| Complexity | Research | Simple level | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner | Not started | Not started | None yet |

**Think of it as:** The colleague who always sends the notes: what we decided, who is doing what, by when.

## Overview

A transcript becomes a short record of decisions, owners and dates.

## Who it is for

Anyone who leaves meetings unsure what was agreed.

## What might be involved at each level

The simple level is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demo | A synthetic transcript and the action record it produces. | Beginner |
| Simple | Paste or attach a transcript and ask for a fixed shape: decisions, actions with an owner and a date, open questions. Check names and dates against the transcript before sharing. | Beginner |
| Working | Runs from your meeting recorder and files each record in one place. | Intermediate |
| Automated | Actions are created in your task tracker and followed up before the next meeting. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | Retention rules, consent to record, and access by team. | Explained, not built here |
