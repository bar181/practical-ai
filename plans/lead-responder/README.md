# Lead responder

A fast, qualified reply to every new enquiry, drafted for approval.

| Complexity | Research | Simple level | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner to advanced | Not started | Not started | None yet |

**Think of it as:** A sharp front-desk colleague who answers every enquiry within the hour, asks the two questions that matter, and passes the hot ones straight to you.

## Overview

A fast, qualified reply to every new enquiry, drafted in your voice for you to approve.

## Who it is for

Any business that loses enquiries because nobody replied quickly.

## What might be involved at each level

The simple level is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demo | Three synthetic enquiries with the reply drafted for each. | Beginner |
| Simple | Write down what makes an enquiry a good fit, and one reply you are proud of. Paste each new enquiry into a chatbot Project that holds both. It drafts the reply and says whether the enquiry fits. You send it. | Beginner |
| Working | Watches the inbox and web forms, drafts with real meeting times, and waits for approval. | Intermediate |
| Automated | Routes urgent enquiries to a person at once and records each one in your customer system. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | Response time targets, routing rules by team, and reporting. | Explained, not built here |

## Established best practices

Lead and outreach skills already available in chat assistants. Email and calendar connectors.

2. Write the simple level solution and its overview.

The working level and beyond are planned only after the simple level is in use.
