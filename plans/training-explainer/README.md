# Training explainer with avatar

Turn a topic into a taught video in a teaching style chosen from a menu.

**Think of it as:** A cover band's set list. The song stays the same. The arrangement changes how it lands.

## Overview

A brief and a menu of teaching styles that turn one topic into a script and a visual plan, then into a video with your avatar.

## Who it is for

Instructors, course builders and anyone who explains things for a living.

## What might be involved at each level

The simple level is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demo | One topic written as two beat sheets, and one finished video per style. | Beginner |
| Simple | Fill in a five-line brief: topic, audience, three to five core areas, style, length. Pick one of five style cards, each with a named inspiration. Write the last line first, build a beat sheet, write the script to it, and record it yourself over slides or stills. | Beginner |
| Working | Script, generated visuals and your own cloned voice, assembled by hand. | Intermediate |
| Automated | An agent produces the whole piece with your avatar and stops at your approval. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | A course-wide style guide, review by a subject expert, captions and translation. | Explained, not built here |
