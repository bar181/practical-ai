# Estimator: landscape review

What established practice says about estimates and quotes, checked on 2026-11-15. The tool was
drafted before this review. Two things in it changed as a result.

## Summary

- **The word on the document matters.** Sources agree that an estimate is an approximation and a
  quote is a fixed price that can bind the business once it is accepted. They do not agree on the
  details, and the rules differ by place. The skill now uses the business's own word and marks
  every draft as a draft.
- **Three options is the common advice for service work.** Many trade guides recommend a basic,
  a middle and a premium option side by side. The estimator prices one job. Options belong to
  the proposal recipe in the artifact studio, which already shows three.
- **Quoting software already does the arithmetic.** If a business has it, the estimator adds
  little. The estimator is for a business that prices from a list and a page of rules.

## Established practice

| Practice | What the sources say | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Estimate and quote are different documents | An estimate is an approximation and is usually not binding. A quote is a fixed price for a defined scope | [FreshBooks](https://www.freshbooks.com/hub/pages/are-estimates-binding), [GoCardless](https://gocardless.com/en-us/guides/posts/difference-between-a-quote-and-estimate/), [Lawyer Monthly](https://www.lawyer-monthly.com/2023/09/whats-the-legal-difference-between-an-estimate-a-quote-and-an-invoice/) |
| The wrong word can commit you | A rough number sent under the word "quote" can lock in a price. A bid mislabelled as an estimate may be treated as an offer | [Agiled](https://agiled.app/hub/agencies/estimate-vs-quote), [FreshBooks](https://www.freshbooks.com/hub/pages/are-estimates-binding) |
| People use the words loosely | Definitions differ between owners and trades, so ask what the customer expects | [FreshBooks](https://freshbooks.com/blog/?p=57641) |
| Put scope, exclusions and an expiry date on a firm price | A binding quote belongs in a signable document that states all three | [Agiled](https://agiled.app/hub/agencies/estimate-vs-quote) |
| Offer three options | Three service levels side by side, each a complete result, so the customer chooses between your options | [Jobber](https://academy.getjobber.com/good-better-best-pricing), [Joist](https://www.joist.com/blog/good-better-best-pricing-contractors/), [ACHR News](https://achrnews.com/articles/155337) |
| The lowest option must still be sound | The basic option has to be safe, adequate, profitable and work you will stand behind | [ShiftFlow](https://www.shiftflow.app/playbook/should-i-include-options-in-quote) |

**Read this with care.** These are software vendors' guides and trade articles. They are not
legal advice, and they do not agree with each other on when a quote binds. What binds a business
depends on where it is and on what was said and signed. A business should settle its own wording
with its own adviser. Claims in these guides about higher sales from three options come from the
vendors themselves and are not repeated here.

## How this tool follows it

| Practice | In this tool |
| --- | --- |
| Estimate and quote are different | **Changed after this review.** The skill asks which word the business uses and uses it throughout. It no longer treats the two as the same |
| The wrong word can commit you | **Changed after this review.** Every draft is headed as a first draft that is not an offer. The skill already never sends and never addresses the customer |
| Scope, exclusions and expiry | The output already lists the job as understood, what was not priced, and the terms copied from the rules. **Added:** if the rules give no validity period, that goes under "Questions before sending" |
| Three options | Not in the estimator, by design. It prices one job from the list. The studio's proposal recipe presents three options |

## What already exists

Not reviewed in detail. Field-service and invoicing products include quoting, and several
advertise three-option quotes. Their prices and limits were not checked. A business that already
uses one should use its quoting, and needs this tool only for the checking habit: every line
traced to a price and a rule.
