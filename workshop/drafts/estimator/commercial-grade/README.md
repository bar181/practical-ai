# Commercial grade: what a commercial build adds

This folder explains. It never holds a project.

## What changes

| Area | In this repository | In a commercial build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prices | One file with a date | A governed price book with owners, effective dates and history |
| Rules | A numbered list | Versioned rules with tests that prove each one still gives the expected total |
| Audit | You checked three lines | A record of every estimate: inputs, rule versions, who approved |
| Systems | Files | Connected to accounting, customer records and e-signature |
| People | One estimator | Roles: who may draft, who may discount, who may send |

## What does not change

- No invented facts.
- A person approves anything that goes out.

## Signs you have outgrown the levels here

- Two people quoted the same job at different prices
- A customer disputes a quote and you cannot show how it was built
- A price changed and old quotes went out anyway
