# Level working: estimator

The common size. What most people use day to day.

**Think of it as** a junior estimator in their second year: they know your past jobs and ask better questions.

**Status: outline.** Written from the simple level. Not yet used by anyone.

## What this level adds

Past quotes as reference, checks on the request before pricing, and a standing list of the questions your jobs always raise.

## One-time setup

1. Add five to ten past quotes to the Project, with private details removed. Mark each with what happened: won, lost, went over.
2. Write an input checklist for your trade: the quantities and choices a request must contain before it can be priced.
3. Keep the price list in one file with a date at the top. Change the date whenever a price changes.
4. Number your rules and never renumber them. Add new rules at the end.

## Each time

1. Paste the request. The estimator restates the job and asks what is missing.
2. Answer the questions, or tell it to proceed and list its assumptions.
3. Check the arithmetic on three lines yourself.
4. Send the quote yourself, in your own name.

## When to move up a level

Move to the automated level when you do this most weeks and the setup above has stopped changing.
