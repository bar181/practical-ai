# Worked example: kitchen renovation

Synthetic example. Figures are illustrative units. This example is dated 2026.

## The request

> Our kitchen is 12 feet by 12 feet. We want about 20 feet of semi-custom cabinets, a quartz
> counter of roughly 40 square feet, a new floor and a tile backsplash of about 30 square feet.
> The sink can stay where it is. We would like 6 new ceiling lights. The house was built in 1985.

## The estimate

**Job as understood**

A full kitchen renovation in a 144 square foot room (12 by 12): 20 linear feet of semi-custom
cabinets, 40 square feet of quartz countertop, new flooring, 30 square feet of backsplash, sink in
its current position, 6 new lights. The house is 41 years old.

**Adjustments**

- Project management, rule 4: 8% of 26,028 = 2,082.24. Running total 28,110.24.
- Contingency, rule 5: the house is more than 30 years old, so 10% of 28,110.24 = 2,811.02. Running total 30,921.26.

**Total**

30,900 (exact figure 30,921.26, rounded to the nearest 100 under rule 6).

This is a range guide from a description. A firm quote needs a site visit (rule 8).

**Assumptions**

- Floor area is the full room: 12 by 12 feet = 144 square feet.
- The six lights are new fixtures on existing wiring routes.

**Not priced**

- Appliances (rule 7).
- Painting (rule 7).

**Questions before sending**

1. Are new appliances part of the job, and who supplies them?
2. Do the walls or ceiling need painting afterwards?
3. When can we visit to measure and check the wiring and plumbing?

**Terms**

30% on signing, 40% at the midpoint, 30% on completion. Valid for 30 days.
