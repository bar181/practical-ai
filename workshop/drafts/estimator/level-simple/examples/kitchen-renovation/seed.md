# Seed: kitchen renovation

A generic renovation company that prices by measure. Unit prices include labour. Figures are
illustrative units, not market prices.

## Price list

| Item | Unit | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Demolition and disposal | Flat | 2,500 | One kitchen |
| Cabinets, stock | Linear foot | 450 | Installed |
| Cabinets, semi-custom | Linear foot | 750 | Installed |
| Countertop, laminate | Square foot | 35 | Installed |
| Countertop, quartz | Square foot | 90 | Installed |
| Flooring | Square foot | 12 | Installed |
| Backsplash tile | Square foot | 30 | Installed |
| Plumbing, standard hookup | Flat | 600 | Sink stays where it is |
| Plumbing, relocate sink | Flat | 1,200 | Sink moves |
| Electrical, light or outlet | Each | 150 | New fixture or outlet |
| Permits | Flat | 800 | Per job |

## Rules

1. Every job includes demolition and disposal, and permits.
2. Every job includes exactly one plumbing line: standard hookup if the sink stays, relocation if it moves.
3. Flooring is priced on the full floor area of the room.
4. Add project management at 8% of the subtotal of all line items.
5. Add contingency on the subtotal plus project management: 10% if the house is more than 30 years old, otherwise 5%.
6. Round the final total to the nearest 100.
7. Appliances and painting are not on the price list. Never price them. List them as not priced.
8. Never give a firm quote without a site visit. Every estimate from a description is a range guide and must say so.
9. Payment: 30% on signing, 40% at the midpoint, 30% on completion.
10. An estimate is valid for 30 days.
