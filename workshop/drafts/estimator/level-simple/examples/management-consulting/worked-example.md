# Worked example: management consulting

Synthetic example. Figures are illustrative units.

## The request

> We are a 40-person organisation. We want a review of how we bring new customers on board. We
> think it needs some interviews with staff, one workshop with the leadership team, and a written
> report with recommendations. We would like it done within six weeks.

## The estimate

**Job as understood**

A review of the customer onboarding process for a 40-person organisation: staff interviews, one
leadership workshop, and a written report, within six weeks. The number of interviews is not stated.

**Line items**

| Item | Quantity | Unit | Price | Amount | From |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | 3 | Lead consultant days | 1,800 | 5,400 | Rule 1 |
| Interviews and analysis | 6 | Consultant days | 1,200 | 7,200 | Rule 4 |
| Data analysis | 8 | Analyst days | 800 | 6,400 | Rule 4 |
| Workshop, lead | 2 | Lead consultant days | 1,800 | 3,600 | Rule 2 |
| Workshop, consultant | 2 | Consultant days | 1,200 | 2,400 | Rule 2 |
| Report, lead | 2 | Lead consultant days | 1,800 | 3,600 | Rule 3 |
| Report, consultant | 3 | Consultant days | 1,200 | 3,600 | Rule 3 |
| **Labour subtotal** | | | | **32,200** | |

**Adjustments**

- Project management, rule 5: 10% of 32,200 = 3,220. Running total 35,420.
- Contingency, rule 6: the number of interviews is not fixed, so 10% of 35,420 = 3,542. Running total 38,962.

**Total**

39,000 (exact figure 38,962, rounded to the nearest 100 under rule 7).

**Assumptions**

- About 12 staff interviews, which is what 6 consultant days allows for.
- One workshop, with the leadership team.
- The work fits within six weeks at these day counts.

**Not priced**

- Travel and other expenses. These are charged at cost (rule 8).
- Any work to carry out the recommendations.

**Questions before sending**

1. How many people should be interviewed, and are they in one location?
2. Is customer data available for the analyst, and in what form?
3. Who needs to attend the workshop?

**Terms**

40% at the start, 60% on delivery of the report. Valid for 30 days.
