# Seed: employment services

A generic staffing firm that places full-time (permanent) and temporary staff. Figures are
illustrative units, not market prices.

## Price list

### Permanent placement: fee as a share of first-year base salary

| Role level | Fee | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 18% | Most operational and administrative roles |
| Specialist | 22% | Roles needing a scarce skill or licence |
| Senior | 25% | Management and executive roles |

### Temporary staff: hourly bill rate = hourly pay rate x multiplier

| Skill level | Multiplier | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General | 1.45 | No specific qualification |
| Skilled | 1.55 | Trade or certified skill |
| Specialist | 1.65 | Scarce or licensed skill |

## Rules

1. The client states the pay rate for temporary staff and the base salary for permanent roles. Never suggest or assume either.
2. Temporary cost = number of workers x weeks x hours per week x bill rate.
3. Overtime is billed at 1.5 times the bill rate. Only include it if the client gives overtime hours.
4. When a single request has 3 or more permanent placements, reduce the fee for each by 2 percentage points.
5. Do not round. Temporary staff are invoiced weekly on actual hours, so show the weekly figure too.
6. A permanent placement carries a 90-day replacement guarantee. Say so.
7. If a temporary worker is hired permanently by the client, a conversion fee applies. It is not on this price list. List it as not priced.
8. Payment: temporary staff invoiced weekly, payable in 14 days. Permanent fees are due on the start date.
9. An estimate is valid for 30 days.
