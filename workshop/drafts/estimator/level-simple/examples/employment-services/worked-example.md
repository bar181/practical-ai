# Worked example: employment services

Synthetic example. Figures are illustrative units.

## The request

> We need 6 temporary warehouse staff for 8 weeks to cover our busy season, working 40 hours a
> week. We pay 20.00 an hour for this work. We also want to hire one permanent operations
> supervisor at a salary of 70,000.

## The estimate

**Job as understood**

Two parts. Temporary: 6 general warehouse workers for 8 weeks at 40 hours a week, pay rate 20.00
an hour. Permanent: one operations supervisor at a base salary of 70,000, treated as a standard role.

**Line items**

| Item | Quantity | Unit | Price | Amount | From |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Temporary staff, general | 1,920 | Hours | 29.00 | 55,680 | Rule 2 |
| Permanent placement, standard | 1 | Placement | 18% of 70,000 | 12,600 | Price list |

**Adjustments**

- Bill rate: 20.00 x 1.45 = 29.00 an hour (general multiplier).
- Hours: 6 workers x 8 weeks x 40 hours = 1,920 hours. 1,920 x 29.00 = 55,680.
- Weekly temporary invoice: 6 x 40 x 29.00 = 6,960 a week for 8 weeks.
- Permanent fee: 18% of 70,000 = 12,600. One placement, so the volume reduction in rule 4 does not apply.

**Total**

68,280 (55,680 temporary plus 12,600 permanent). Not rounded, under rule 5.

**Assumptions**

- Warehouse work is at the general skill level.
- The supervisor is a standard role, not a senior one.
- No overtime.

**Not priced**

- Overtime, because no overtime hours were given (rule 3).
- A conversion fee if a temporary worker is hired permanently (rule 7).

**Questions before sending**

1. Is any overtime or weekend work expected during the busy season?
2. Does the work need any certificate or licence? That would change the skill level.
3. Does the supervisor role manage a team and a budget? That may make it a senior role.
4. What start date is needed for each part?

**Terms**

Temporary staff invoiced weekly, payable in 14 days. The permanent fee is due on the start date and
carries a 90-day replacement guarantee. Valid for 30 days.
