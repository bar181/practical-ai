# Voice post drafter: student guide

By the end you will have a voice file of your own, a chatbot set up to draft from it, and one post
you edited and are willing to publish.

## The idea in one minute

Describing your voice with adjectives does not work. "Friendly and professional" describes
everyone. Three posts you actually wrote describe you. So the drafter learns from examples, obeys
your stated rules, and gets better because you tell it what you liked and what you did not.

## Step 1: Build your voice file

Copy [`level-simple/voice-file-template.md`](level-simple/voice-file-template.md) and fill in three parts:

1. **Examples:** three to five posts you wrote and still like, pasted in full.
2. **Rules:** words and moves you never use, and things you always do.
3. **Facts I may state:** the claims, numbers and offers that are true and yours to share.

## Step 2: Set up the drafter

Create a Project in your chatbot. Paste
[`level-simple/project-instructions.md`](level-simple/project-instructions.md) into its
instructions and attach your voice file.

## Step 3: Give it a point of view, not a topic

"AI agents" is a topic. "Most small teams need a checklist, not an agent" is a post.

One sentence: the point you want to make, and who it is for. If your idea is vague, ask for two
angles and pick one.

## Step 4: Get one draft for one channel

Ask for a single channel at a time. A newsletter piece cut down is not a social post.

The drafter will show its draft, list which traits of your voice it used, and flag anything it was
unsure you would say.

## Step 5: Edit it and post it yourself

Read it aloud. Change what does not sound like you. **You edit and you publish.** The goal is a
strong first draft, not automation of your name.

## Step 6: Close the loop

Tell the drafter one thing you liked or did not, in plain words:

- "I like this: short lines and it ends on the joke."
- "I don't like this: I never open with a question."

It will give you a line to add to the rules in your voice file. Add it. This step is what makes
draft twenty sound like you. See [`level-demo/example.md`](level-demo/example.md).

## Rules the drafter follows

1. Rules in the voice file are read before anything is written.
2. One idea per post.
3. Facts come from the voice file or from you. Results, numbers and client stories are never invented.
4. If your request conflicts with one of your own rules, it says so and asks.
5. Nothing is posted for you, at any level.

## Try it

1. Build the voice file from three posts.
2. Draft one post on something you explained to someone this week.
3. Give one line of feedback, add it to the rules, and draft a second post. Compare.
