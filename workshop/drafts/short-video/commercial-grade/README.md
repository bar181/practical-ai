# Commercial grade: what a commercial build adds

This folder explains. It never holds a project.

## What changes

| Area | In this repository | In a commercial build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand | Your caption style on a page | A brand kit applied to every render: type, colour, intro and outro |
| Likeness and voice | Your own, by your say-so | Written consent, licences and expiry dates tracked for every person and voice |
| Review | You watch it once | A reviewer checks facts, captions and disclosure labels |
| Volume | One at a time | Batches from a content calendar, with versions per channel |
| Rights | Clips you made | Licences for music, footage and fonts recorded per video |

## What does not change

- No invented facts.
- A person approves anything that goes out.

## Signs you have outgrown the levels here

- A video went out with someone's likeness and nobody can find the consent
- The same video looks different on each channel
- You cannot say what a month of video cost
