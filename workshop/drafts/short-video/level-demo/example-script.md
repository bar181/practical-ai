# Example: a filled-in 20-second script

Synthetic example. Topic: why a checklist beats memory.

| Beat | Seconds | Spoken | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0 to 2 | You do not have a memory problem. | "Not a memory problem" |
| Point | 2 to 12 | You have a writing-it-down problem. The step you skip is always the one you were sure you would remember. | A checklist with one box unticked |
| Proof | 12 to 17 | I wrote five steps on a card. I have not missed one in a month. | "5 steps. 30 days. 0 misses." |
| Ask | 17 to 20 | Write your five tonight. | "Write your five" |

Spoken word count: 45. Read aloud at a normal pace: about 18 seconds, which leaves room for a pause after the hook.

| Setting | Choice |
| --- | --- |
| Route | C, avatar short |
| Shape | Vertical |
| Caption style | Large white text on a dark band, two lines at most |
| The one thing the viewer should do | Write a five-step checklist |
