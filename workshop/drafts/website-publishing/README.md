# Publishing a website

Take a page an assistant built and put it where other people can open it, in three steps of
rising effort.

**Think of it as** moving out of a sketchbook. First you show the page to a friend. Then you hang
it in a gallery. Then you set up a studio that hangs each new piece for you.

**North Star:** a page that works on your screen is at an address you chose, seen only by the
people you meant, within ten minutes, and you know how to take it down.

**Who it is for:** anyone who has built a page with an assistant and wants to share it.

## Three routes

| Route | Effort | You get | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Share the link | Minutes, nothing to install | A link to the page inside your assistant | A few people need to see it, soon |
| 2. Upload to a host | About 15 minutes, a free account | A public address, and your own domain if you want one | It should stay up and look like yours |
| 3. Repository that deploys itself | An hour once | Every approved change goes live, with history and previews | The page will keep changing, or more than one person edits it |
