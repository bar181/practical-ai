# Meeting to actions

A transcript becomes decisions, owners and dates.

| Complexity | Research | Level 1 | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner | Not started | Not started | None yet |

**Think of it as:** The colleague who always sends the notes: what we decided, who is doing what, by when.

## Overview

A transcript becomes a short record of decisions, owners and dates.

## Who it is for

Anyone who leaves meetings unsure what was agreed.

## Scope

- **In:** the Beginner level described below.
- **Out:** the Advanced level and beyond, until the Beginner level is in use.
- **It works when:** to be written before anything is built.

## What might be involved at each level

Level 1 is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

Level 1 is the Beginner level: a student can run it in any chat assistant with nothing installed. Levels 2 and 3 are the Advanced levels, with skills and connectors, for someone who works with agents every day.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base | A synthetic transcript and the action record it produces. | Beginner |
| Level 1, Beginner: for students | Paste or attach a transcript and ask for a fixed shape: decisions, actions with an owner and a date, open questions. Check names and dates against the transcript before sharing. | Beginner |
| Level 2, Advanced: skills and connectors | A skill that reads the transcript from your meeting recorder through a connector (Granola and Zoom each have one), writes actions to a sheet and drafts the follow-up email. | Intermediate |
| Level 3, Expert: automation | Actions are created in your task tracker and followed up before the next meeting. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | Retention rules, consent to record, and access by team. | Explained, not built here |
