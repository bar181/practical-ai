# Lead responder

A fast, qualified reply to every new enquiry, drafted for approval.

| Complexity | Research | Level 1 | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner to advanced | Not started | Not started | None yet |

**Think of it as:** A sharp front-desk colleague who answers every enquiry within the hour, asks the two questions that matter, and passes the hot ones straight to you.

## Overview

A fast, qualified reply to every new enquiry, drafted in your voice for you to approve.

## Who it is for

Any business that loses enquiries because nobody replied quickly.

## Scope

- **In:** the Beginner level described below.
- **Out:** the Advanced level and beyond, until the Beginner level is in use.
- **It works when:** to be written before anything is built.

## What might be involved at each level

Level 1 is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

Level 1 is the Beginner level: a student can run it in any chat assistant with nothing installed. Levels 2 and 3 are the Advanced levels, with skills and connectors, for someone who works with agents every day.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base | Three synthetic enquiries with the reply drafted for each. | Beginner |
| Level 1, Beginner: for students | Write down what makes an enquiry a good fit, and one reply you are proud of. Paste each new enquiry into a chatbot Project that holds both. It drafts the reply and says whether the enquiry fits. You send it. | Beginner |
| Level 2, Advanced: skills and connectors | A skill with a Gmail connector that watches the inbox and web forms, drafts with real meeting times from the calendar, logs each lead to a sheet and waits for approval. | Intermediate |
| Level 3, Expert: automation | Routes urgent enquiries to a person at once and records each one in your customer system. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | Response time targets, routing rules by team, and reporting. | Explained, not built here |
