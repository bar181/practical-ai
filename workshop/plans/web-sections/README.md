# Plan: Web Sections

Written on 21 November 2026 as part of [the Artifact Studio split](../artifact-studio-split/README.md).

**Think of it as** a kit of rooms: choose the rooms a house needs, in the right order, and leave
the paint for later.

**North Star:** anyone starting a website or page gets the right sections in the right order,
responsive and accessible, filled with their own content and clear placeholders, so that neither
they nor their AI starts from a blank file.

| Heading | Write here |
| --- | --- |
| The job | Pick the page type and sections from what the page must do; assemble them from a tested library; fill them; leave visible placeholders |
| The user | Small business owners, course creators and anyone whose AI keeps building sites from scratch |
| What is out | Effects and finished visual design (Artifact Studio). Tone and copy (Tone Studio). Hosting (Site Studio) |
| What already exists | Many free section libraries and site builders. The owner's call: this is not meant to compete or be state of the art. Its value is one single-file library an AI can copy from, with a "use when" on every section and a skill that chooses |
| The design | `base/template/sections.html` (six heroes and twelve more sections, five assemblies), social post sizes, one skill |
| Levels | 1: paste the skill and build. 2: your own saved section set. 3: chained with Tone Studio and Artifact Studio (planned) |
| Who reviewed it | Fresh agents on two invented cases. [Evidence](../../../projects/web-sections/quality-results/README.md). No person yet |
