# Website chatbot

A site assistant that answers from your own notes and captures enquiries.

**Think of it as:** A receptionist who knows your price list and your opening hours, takes a message when unsure, and never makes a promise you did not write down.

## Overview

A site assistant that answers visitors from your own notes and captures enquiries.

## Who it is for

Any business whose website gets the same ten questions.

## Scope

- **In:** the Beginner level described below.
- **Out:** the Advanced level and beyond, until the Beginner level is in use.
- **It works when:** to be written before anything is built.

## What might be involved at each level

Level 1 is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

Level 1 is the Beginner level: a student can run it in any chat assistant with nothing installed. Levels 2 and 3 are the Advanced levels, with skills and connectors, for someone who works with agents every day.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base | A sample assistant answering from a synthetic set of notes. | Beginner |
| Level 1, Beginner: for students | Write one page of facts: what you offer, prices you publish, hours, how to reach you, and the ten questions you hear most. Load it into a chatbot Project and test it with awkward questions before it goes near a website. | Beginner |
| Level 2, Advanced: skills and connectors | Embedded on the site, answering only from notes kept in a connected Google Drive folder or sheet, and writing a name and a question to a sheet when it cannot answer. | Intermediate |
| Level 3, Expert: automation | Books meetings, hands a conversation to a person, and records enquiries in your customer system. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | Conversation records, consent and privacy notices, languages, and uptime monitoring. | Explained, not built here |
