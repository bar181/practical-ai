# The note

## Decided
From your message:
- The user is you, the owner of a kitchen-fitting company with six staff.
- The job: every Monday, last week's sales notes become an email for the team.
- The notes are a few lines typed on your phone during the week.
- You use ChatGPT on your phone and laptop. You are not technical, so the first version is a prompt to paste, with no code.

## Assumed
You could not be asked, so these were decided for you. Change any of them.
- Short path rather than all nine steps: the job is one person, one kind of input, one result, inside a chat, and it needs no files, nothing paid, and nobody else using the tool. The staff only receive the email.
- Assistant: ChatGPT, the one you already use.
- Where the prompt lives: a note on your phone.
- Tone: plain and friendly. Length: under 200 words. Shape: subject line, one opening sentence, "Last week" and "This week" lists, sign-off.
- Unclear notes come back as questions, never guesses.
- The proposed wow: a sendable draft in under a minute, with nothing made up.
- Step 1 gate ("Can you say the job in one sentence?"): yes. "Every Monday, turn last week's sales notes into a draft team email I read and send."

## Test this next
1. **Try one plain sentence first.** Open a new ChatGPT chat and type: "Turn these sales notes into a short email to my team:" then paste last week's notes. If you would send the result unchanged, stop: you do not need the prompt.
2. **The fresh-chat test.** Open a new ChatGPT chat with nothing else in it (if your ChatGPT has a chat option that does not use memory, use that). Paste the whole prompt from the note. Under "My notes:", paste the four invented example lines above. Send. Do not add anything else.
3. **Check the result** against the six-point checklist above. Write down every point it missed and anything it tripped on, for example: did it guess about Sam and the Harris job, add a price, or go over 200 words?
4. **Then try it on your real notes** from last week. Note every change you had to make before you would send it.
5. If it misses a point, change one rule in the prompt at a time and run step 2 again.
