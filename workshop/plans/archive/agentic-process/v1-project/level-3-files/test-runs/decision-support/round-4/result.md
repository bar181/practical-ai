# The brief

**Decision:** Should Harbour Lane Bakery start a weekday catering service for offices near its three shops?

**Recommendation:** Run an 8-week, $3,000-capped trial of office catering (at most 10 orders a week, bread, pastries and coffee only, delivered in the weekday afternoon), and decide on a full service after it.

**Why:**
- It is the only option that adds sales and afternoon sales without breaking a limit: a full service would add to the head baker's hours (assumed), and its costs were not found.
- Local office demand was not found. Ontario civil servants are back in offices five days a week [1][2] and national office vacancy is falling [3], but nothing shows whether offices near your shops would order. A cheap trial answers that.
- Your risk is medium and the facts are not strong, so a capped step fits. Local caterers' $15 to $26 per person menus [4] give a price reference.

**SWOT:**

| | Helpful | Harmful |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Internal** | No new building needed (profile). Products already made daily (profile). Loss capped at $3,000 (proposal). | New work for a team at risk of burnout (profile). Head baker has no spare days (profile). Narrower menu than lunch caterers (assumed). |
| **External** | Ontario civil servants back in office five days a week [1][2]. Office vacancy falling nationally [3]. Local price reference of $15 to $26 per person [4]. | Established local caterers [4]. Marketplace fees of 15 to 25 percent [5]. Transport temperature rules [6]. Local demand not found. |

**Risks and how to reduce them:**
1. *Staff overload.* Cap orders at 10 a week and pack only in the afternoon lull (proposal). Stop the trial if any shop reports overtime.
2. *Food safety in transit.* Keep the trial menu to bread, pastries and coffee, and ask Hamilton Public Health which items need temperature control on the way [6].
3. *Weak demand.* Do the phone checks before spending anything.

**Next steps (next 30 days):**
1. Agree the trial terms (length, cap, menu, success measure) with your partner.
2. Call or visit 10 offices within walking distance of each shop. Ask whether they order food for meetings, how often, and what they pay per person.
3. Call Hamilton Public Health about catering from your existing shops, and your insurance broker about delivery.

**What would change this recommendation:** At least 5 paid orders a week by week 6 with repeat offices, and a production lead other than the head baker, would justify costing a full service. Few interested offices in the calls would mean "not now".

**Assumptions:** "Head baker already works six days" means no added hours. Afternoons are your quieter period. Bread and pastries are simpler to transport safely than meals. Trial length, cap, order limit and success measure are proposals.
