## 5. The brief

**Decision:** Should Harbour Lane Bakery start a weekday catering service for offices near its shops?

**Recommendation:** Run an 8-week afternoon pastry-and-coffee pilot for offices from one shop, capped at $3,000, after first checking baking capacity and office interest.

**Why:**
- It scored highest of three options (+2, against 0 for a full service and 0 for doing nothing), mainly because it targets your weekday afternoon goal without adding work across all three shops.
- It stays well inside your $25,000 limit and needs no new building.
- Hamilton office demand could not be found, so a small test costs far less than learning the same thing with a full launch.

**SWOT**

Food must stay at 4°C or below, or 60°C or above, in transit (O. Reg. 493/17). |

**Risks and how to reduce them:**
1. **The head baker gets pulled in.** Rule (proposal): the pilot stops if the head baker's hours would rise.
2. **Few afternoon orders.** Call offices before spending; agree a stop number with your partner, for example 10 orders a week by week 4 (proposal).
3. **Food safety and delivery cover.** Ask a Hamilton Public Health inspector what applies to delivered food, and your insurance broker whether staff can deliver by car. Until then, offer pick-up only (proposal).

**Next steps (next 30 days):**
1. Ask your bakers what extra the chosen shop can make each weekday without the head baker.
2. Call or visit about ten offices near that shop: would they order an afternoon pastry and coffee box, how often, at what price?
3. Ask your accountant how HST applies to catered bakery items, and your insurance broker about staff deliveries.

**What would change this recommendation:** Little office interest, or extra baking that needs the head baker, means do nothing. Steady weekly orders and baking capacity to spare mean consider the full service.

**Assumptions:**
- "The head baker already works six days" means no option may add to the head baker's hours.
- Offices exist within reach of at least one shop (not found).
- Pilot length, cap, menu, delivery window and stop number are my proposals for you and your partner to change.
