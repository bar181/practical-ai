# Example 1: just the email

**What this is:** a reference, hand-written by the building agent by following
[`AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md`](../AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md) step by step. It is not a ChatGPT output. Use it to
judge what your assistant gives you.

**Kind of request:** the owner pastes the customer email and nothing else, so every fact and
decision becomes a `[needs: ...]` blank.

## What the owner pasted

```
Subject: VERY disappointed - order #4471

Hi,

I am honestly so frustrated I don't even know where to start. I ordered the sage green linen
tablecloth from your shop on the 2nd of this month because it was supposed to be a birthday
present for my mother. Your website said 3 to 5 days. It took TWELVE days. The birthday was on
Saturday and I had nothing to give her, which was embarrassing in front of the whole family.

And then when it finally arrived, it is not even sage green. It's a kind of grey-blue. I double
checked my order confirmation and it clearly says sage green. So not only was it late, it's the
wrong item.

I emailed you twice last week (on Tuesday and again on Thursday) and nobody answered. Not even an
automatic reply. Is anyone actually running this business? I have bought from you three times
before and always recommended you to friends, but I won't be doing that anymore.

I want a full refund, including the shipping I paid for, and I want to know what you are going to
do about the wrong colour, because I am not paying to send it back. If I don't hear back by
Friday I will be leaving a review on Google telling people exactly what happened.

Dana Whitfield
```
