# Replaced

`project-one-through-the-nine-steps.md` was the third worked example for Agentic Process. It was
retired on 16 November 2026: the owner asked for the project's guidance to stand on its own,
without examples taken from how this repository was built. Each step now has a purpose, user
stories, proof and a prompt in [`base/PROCESS.md`](../../../../projects/agentic-process/base/PROCESS.md).

`training-30-minutes.html`, `TEACH-IN-30-MINUTES.md` and `lesson.py` were the 30-minute lesson
and its source. They were retired on 16 November 2026 when the owner asked for a full course:
[`base/course.html`](../../../../projects/agentic-process/base/course.html), with modules, lessons and a
lecture deck per module. The 30-minute version lives on as the "taster" in the course's
instructor guide.

`course-v1.py` was the source of the first full course: five modules on building AI tools in
general, with a lecture deck per module. It was retired on 16 November 2026 when the owner asked
for a course about this project itself: how it works, why each step exists, and how to run it,
told visually. Its replacement is written from `_build/course.py` into the same file,
[`base/course.html`](../../../../projects/agentic-process/base/course.html).

## Version 1, archived on 16 November 2026

`v1-project/` is the whole of Agentic Process version 1, as it stood in `projects/agentic-process/`,
and `v1-plan/` holds its spec, Autopilot tracker and last hand-back. They were replaced by version
2, a restart in [`workshop/drafts/agentic-process/`](../../../drafts/agentic-process/) with a new
spec. Version 2 carries over the nine steps, the builder skill with its template and checks,
Autopilot, the fair-test rules, the Base package template and the decision-support tool. Why:
[`EVALUATION.md`](../../../drafts/agentic-process/EVALUATION.md). Links inside these folders are
left as they were.

## Version 2 restart notes and dropped parts, archived on 16 November 2026

`v2-restart/` holds what the simpler version 2 outline dropped: the evaluation and lessons from
the restart, Autopilot, the test rules, the builder's project template and its scaffolding script,
the package template, and decision support's separate workflow card and instruction file.
Replaced by one `SKILL.md` per tool, the template in `workshop/drafts/agentic-process/template/`,
and the one-page `PROCESS.md`.

## Build process guide, archived on 16 November 2026

`BUILD-PROCESS-2026-11-15.md` was the nine-step build guide in the plans template. Replaced by
`projects/agentic-process/base/PROCESS.md`; the template keeps a short pointer.
