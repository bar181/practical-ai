# Replaced

`project-one-through-the-nine-steps.md` was the third worked example for Agentic Process. It was
retired on 16 November 2026: the owner asked for the project's guidance to stand on its own,
without examples taken from how this repository was built. Each step now has a purpose, user
stories, proof and a prompt in [`base/PROCESS.md`](../../../../projects/agentic-process/base/PROCESS.md).

`training-30-minutes.html`, `TEACH-IN-30-MINUTES.md` and `lesson.py` were the 30-minute lesson
and its source. They were retired on 16 November 2026 when the owner asked for a full course:
[`base/course.html`](../../../../projects/agentic-process/base/course.html), with modules, lessons and a
lecture deck per module. The 30-minute version lives on as the "taster" in the course's
instructor guide.
