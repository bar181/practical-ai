# Training website

A course site with lessons, a schedule and a built-in assistant.

| Complexity | Research | Level 1 | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner to advanced | Not started | Not started | None yet |

**Think of it as:** A classroom noticeboard that is always up to date: this week's lesson, what is due, where the materials are.

## Overview

A course site with lessons, a schedule and resources in one place, with a course assistant built in.

## Who it is for

Instructors and course builders.

## Scope

- **In:** the Beginner level described below.
- **Out:** the Advanced level and beyond, until the Beginner level is in use.
- **It works when:** to be written before anything is built.

## What might be involved at each level

Level 1 is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

Level 1 is the Beginner level: a student can run it in any chat assistant with nothing installed. Levels 2 and 3 are the Advanced levels, with skills and connectors, for someone who works with agents every day.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base | A sample course site for a synthetic three-lesson course. | Beginner |
| Level 1, Beginner: for students | One page per course built as an artifact: the outline, this week's lesson, what is due, and links to materials. Update it by asking your assistant to revise the page. | Beginner |
| Level 2, Advanced: skills and connectors | A skill that builds lesson pages from a Google Drive folder of materials, with a schedule that updates itself and the student assistant embedded. | Intermediate |
| Level 3, Expert: automation | Sign-in for students, progress tracking, surveys and reminders. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | Enrolment and payments, certificates, accessibility review and support. | Explained, not built here |
