# Plan: Meeting notes to actions

Written on 16 November 2026 by following [Agentic Process](../../../projects/agentic-process/),
steps 1 to 6, with the one-page plan from its template. Step 2 was searched on the web on that
date.
**The owner has not yet answered the gates;** each is marked open where it needs him.

**Think of it as:** the colleague who always sends the notes, and who writes "no owner" in red
when nobody actually agreed to do the thing.

**North Star:** a business owner or employee turns a meeting transcript or rough notes into
decisions, actions and a follow-up message, and sees at once which actions have no owner or no
date because the meeting never set one, in one sitting, with the chatbot they already use.

| Heading | Write here |
| --- | --- |
| The job | Turn one meeting transcript or set of notes into decisions, actions with an owner and a date, open questions, and a short follow-up message. Flag every owner or date the meeting did not set |
| The user | Whoever leaves a meeting and has to send the notes. In class: a learner with an invented transcript |

**Lean for the Beginner level: wrap.** Meeting tools already list actions. What none of them is
built to do is say plainly which actions nobody agreed to own or date. That habit, and the quote
behind each action, is the lesson, and the prompt works on any tool's notes or a raw transcript.
