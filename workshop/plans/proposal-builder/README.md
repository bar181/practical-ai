# Proposal builder

Notes from a client conversation, or a finished estimate, become a formatted proposal.

| Complexity | Research | Level 1 | Draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner to advanced | Not started | Not started | None yet |

**Think of it as:** A document specialist who takes your scribbled notes and your price and returns something you would be glad to sign.

## Overview

Notes from a client conversation, or a finished estimate, become a formatted proposal in your own structure.

## Who it is for

Anyone who writes proposals and reuses the same sections each time.

## Scope

- **In:** the Beginner level described below.
- **Out:** the Advanced level and beyond, until the Beginner level is in use.
- **It works when:** to be written before anything is built.

## What might be involved at each level

Level 1 is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

Level 1 is the Beginner level: a student can run it in any chat assistant with nothing installed. Levels 2 and 3 are the Advanced levels, with skills and connectors, for someone who works with agents every day.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base | A sample proposal produced from synthetic notes and an estimate. | Beginner |
| Level 1, Beginner: for students | Write one proposal template with your standard sections. Give your assistant the template, your notes and the price. It fills in the proposal and lists anything it had to assume. You check it and send it. | Beginner |
| Level 2, Advanced: skills and connectors | A skill that builds the proposal with the artifact studio from templates and past proposals in a connected Google Drive folder, with prices from the estimator. | Intermediate |
| Level 3, Expert: automation | Sent for signature, with a deposit invoice raised when it is accepted. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | Approval for discounts, clause libraries, and tracking of what was sent and signed. | Explained, not built here |
