# Plan: Write and edit in my voice

Written on 16 November 2026 by following [Agentic Process](../../../projects/agentic-process/),
steps 1 to 6, with the one-page plan from its template. It is the classroom version of the
[voice post drafter](../voice-post-drafter/), and reuses its draft and its dated
[landscape review](../../drafts/voice-post-drafter/landscape-review.md). **The owner has not yet
answered the gates;** each is marked open where it needs him.

**Think of it as:** a tailor's pattern. You measure once from three things you wrote, then every
new piece is cut to fit, and you still check the fit in the mirror.

**North Star:** a business owner or employee turns three samples of their own writing into a
short voice guide, uses it to revise one real email or post, and checks that every fact and
promise stayed true, in one sitting, with the chatbot they already use.

## Step 2: what already exists

From the [voice post drafter's landscape review](../../drafts/voice-post-drafter/landscape-review.md),
checked on 15 November 2026 and not checked again here.

- **Claude's styles** build a reusable style from writing samples. For many people that is enough,
  and the review says to try it first.
- **Other assistants** are reported to offer style settings; this was not checked.
- **Established practice:** paired examples for each trait, a words-to-avoid list with an
  alternative beside each, real samples, and a reason with each "don't".

**Lean for the Beginner level: wrap.** A style setting learns the voice. What it does not do is
the part this lesson teaches: write the voice down where the learner can read and correct it, and
check that the revision kept every fact and promise. The second half is the skill that transfers
to any AI writing.
