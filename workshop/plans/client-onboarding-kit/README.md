# Client onboarding kit

A welcome pack and a checklist for each new client, tailored from what you already know about them.

| Complexity | Research | Level 1 | Draft |

**Think of it as:** A hotel welcome folder made for one guest: their name on the cover, their schedule inside.

## Overview

A welcome pack and a checklist for each new client, written from what you already know about them.

## Who it is for

Anyone who starts new client work more than a few times a year.

## Scope

- **In:** the Beginner level described below.
- **Out:** the Advanced level and beyond, until the Beginner level is in use.
- **It works when:** to be written before anything is built.

## What might be involved at each level

Level 1 is described in most detail, because it is built first. The later levels are
outlines and will change.

Level 1 is the Beginner level: a student can run it in any chat assistant with nothing installed. Levels 2 and 3 are the Advanced levels, with skills and connectors, for someone who works with agents every day.

| Level | What it involves | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base | A sample welcome pack and checklist for a synthetic client. | Beginner |
| Level 1, Beginner: for students | Write one welcome template and one checklist. Give your assistant the template and your notes from the sales conversation, and ask for a tailored pack: what happens in week one, who to contact, what you need from them. | Beginner |
| Level 2, Advanced: skills and connectors | A skill that fills the pack from your customer records and the signed proposal through connectors, and puts the checklist dates in the calendar. | Intermediate |
| Level 3, Expert: automation | The kickoff is scheduled and the tasks are created when a deal is marked won. | Advanced |
| Commercial grade | Templates by service line, sign-off steps and a handover record. | Explained, not built here |
