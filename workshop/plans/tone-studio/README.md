# Plan: Tone Studio

Written on 21 November 2026 as part of [the Artifact Studio split](../artifact-studio-split/README.md).

**Think of it as** a speechwriter: the facts are the client's; the order, the first line and the
rhythm are the writer's.

**North Star:** anyone turns their own content into the tone and story order their reader needs (a
pitch, a one-pager, a story, an explainer), and sees that every fact survived, in one sitting, with
the chatbot they already use.

| Heading | Write here |
| --- | --- |
| The job | Choose a tone and a format from the reader and the goal; rewrite the content in that order; check every fact |
| The user | Founders, managers, teachers and small business owners who write to persuade, explain or announce |
| What is out | Design and layout (Artifact Studio). Page structure (Web Sections). Inventing facts |
| What already exists | The seven voice cards and four deck manners inside Artifact Studio, which this reuses. Write and edit in my voice, reused for a person's own voice at Level 2 |
| The design | One skill: seven tones by reader, nine formats, the words in order, a facts check, gaps |
| Levels | 1: paste. 2: a brand tone file or a voice guide. 3: tone plus Artifact Studio design from one intent (planned) |
| Who reviewed it | Fresh agents on three invented cases. [Evidence](../../../projects/tone-studio/quality-results/README.md). No person yet |
| Next | Make Tone Studio the owner of the voice cards (split plan, Phase 2). Add more formats as good examples are found: a grant summary, a job ad, a case study |
