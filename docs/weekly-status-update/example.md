# Example: a freelancer's weekly update to a client

This is what a good result looks like. Made on 23 November 2026 by a fresh Claude session (a
separate agent with no other context) that had only [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) and the message below.
It ran on the version after the second review round. The last small wording fixes (how a carried-over
item is phrased, how words are counted) came after it, so its carried-over lines and word count
follow the earlier wording.
**Everything here is invented:** Oakfield Community Centre, Joan, Alex, PayEasy, GiveBox, the dates
and the figures. Nothing below the line is edited.

**The message:**

```
Can you write this week's update for Joan? She's the centre manager and she decides whether we launch on the date and signs off the content. Last week's update is at the bottom.

My notes this week:
- good week!
- home page and events page built, both on the test site
- booking form: tested 12 bookings, all came through to the office inbox
- hosting transfer from the old provider still not done, they want a signed form from Joan. launch can't happen till the transfer's done. realistically 12 Dec now
- donations page: Joan needs to pick PayEasy or GiveBox, I need it by 1 Dec to have it ready for launch
- next week: events calendar import, mobile fixes
- note to self: renew my own software licence

LAST WEEK'S UPDATE (as sent)

Subject: Oakfield website: on track

Hi Joan,
We are on track to launch on 5 December.
Done: site design approved, test site set up.
Risk: we still need the photos of the hall and garden from the centre. Without them the home page uses stock images.
Next week: home page, events page, booking form. I will also send you the draft privacy page by Wednesday.
Thanks,
Alex
```

---

**Part 1: the update**

```
Subject: Oakfield website: off track

Hi Joan,

Status: Off track (was on track). The launch date has moved from 5 December to 12 December because the hosting transfer is not done.

Decision needed: Donations page: PayEasy or GiveBox. I need your choice by 1 Dec to have the page ready for launch.

What changed since last week:
- Launch date: was 5 December, now realistically 12 December.
- Home page and events page built, both on the test site.
- Booking form: tested 12 bookings, all came through to the office inbox.

Risks and open questions:
- The hosting transfer from the old provider is still not done. The launch can't happen until it is. The old provider wants a signed form from you.

Carried over, no news this week:
- Photos of the hall and garden from the centre. Without them the home page uses stock images.
- Draft privacy page, which I said I would send you by Wednesday.

Next week:
- Events calendar import.
- Mobile fixes.

Thanks,
Alex
```

**Part 2: checks before you send** (not part of the update)

Questions for you:
- **Photos of the hall and garden:** this was a risk last week but your notes don't mention it. Did the centre send them, or will the home page still use stock images?
- **Draft privacy page:** last week you said you'd send it by Wednesday. Did you? If you did, I'll move it to "What changed". Joan signs off the content, so she may need to review it.
- **Hosting transfer:** does 12 December depend on Joan signing the form by a certain date? Your notes don't say. If it does, add that date to the risk so she knows when it's needed. I didn't add one.
- **Launch date:** Joan decides whether you launch on the date. Do you want to ask her to agree to 12 December as the new launch date? I didn't add that as a decision because your notes don't ask her for it.

Status:
- I chose **Off track** because last week you told Joan the launch was 5 December, and your notes now put it at 12 December. "Good week!" doesn't change that. The work on the pages and the booking form did go well, and the update says so.

Assumed or interpreted:
- I read "realistically 12 Dec now" as the new launch date and kept the word "realistically".
- I read "I need it by 1 Dec" as the deadline for Joan's choice of PayEasy or GiveBox.
- I wrote "12 Dec" out as "12 December" so it matches last week's "5 December". "1 Dec" is as you wrote it.
- Your notes don't give the date or the week this covers, so the subject line has no week.
- I used the same greeting and sign-off as last week ("Hi Joan," and "Thanks, Alex").

Left out because they weren't for Joan:
- "good week!": this is a mood, not a fact, so I didn't use it for the status.
- "note to self: renew my own software licence": this is a personal task.

Word count: 163 words, counting the subject line, greeting and sign-off.
