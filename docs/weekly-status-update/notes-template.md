# Weekly notes template

Keep this open during the week and add a line whenever something happens. It takes seconds, and
on Friday you paste it, with last week's update, into a chat with [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md).

Rough is fine. What matters is the dates, the numbers and the exact problem, in your own words.

```
Project: <name>
Reader: <who reads the update>
They decide: <what they decide from it>
Week of: <date>

Done:
- <what finished, and the date if it matters>

Moved (dates or numbers that changed):
- <what> was <old>, now <new>, because <reason, if known>

Problems and risks:
- <what is wrong or could go wrong>, affects <which date or figure>, waiting on <whom or what>

Decisions I need from the reader:
- <the decision>, options: <A or B>, needed by <date>

Still open from last week:
- <a risk, date or promise from last week's update, and any news on it>

Next week:
- <plan>

Not for this reader (but I want to remember):
- <anything else>
```

## Tips

- **Write the old value when something moves.** "Install was 80% by Friday, now 60%" tells the
  reader more than "install 60%".
- **Write the problem, not the feeling.** "Landlord has not confirmed the dock repair; heavy stock
  cannot move without it" beats "a bit worried about the dock".
- **Say if you need a decision.** The tool will not invent one. If you want the reader to choose,
  write it under "Decisions I need".
- **Keep last week's update.** Paste it next time, as sent. It is how the tool spots anything that
  went quiet.

The headings are a help, not a rule. The tool also works on a messy list or pasted messages.
