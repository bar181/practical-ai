Start in two minutes

The idea in one sentence

A lesson is only useful if it comes back before the next time, so this keeps every lesson as one instruction, counts the repeats, and hands the rules back when you start similar work.

Think of it as a ship's log.One line each time the crew hits a rock or finds a faster channel, read by the next crew before they sail. The AI writes the line. You decide which lines become the rules.
  1. Say "log that"
    "The holiday email went out with last year's dates. I expected this year's."
  2. Get one filed row
    error · marketing · check. When reusing a past email, check every date first. Saved after your yes. A repeat? Its count goes up.
  3. The rules come back
    "Before I start the December newsletter": the rules you accepted, ready to follow.

Get started

  1. Copy the prompt30 seconds
    Change the last line to your own lesson. 
    Read the instructions at this link and follow them for the rest of this chat:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bar181/practical-ai/main/projects/learnings-database/base/SKILL.md

log that: [what happened]. I expected [what you expected].
  2. Paste it into your chatbot10 seconds
    Claude, ChatGPT, or another chatbot that can open links. Attach your log if you have one, so it can spot repeats.
  3. Check the row1 minute
    You get the lesson as "When ..., do ...", filed by kind and area. Check it says what happened, in your words.
  4. Keep it30 seconds
    Say "yes, give me the updated file" and save it over your old one. First time? It gives you the starter spreadsheet.

Chatbot cannot open the link? Copy the instructions file into the chat instead: the project page shows how.

Common mistakes

  • Logging opinions. "We should post more" is a plan, not a lesson. It declines those in one line.
  • Forgetting the log. Without your file attached it cannot spot a repeat, and says so.
  • Accepting everything. The five-minute review is the point: accept the rules you will follow, retire the rest.
  • Putting names in rules. Rules name no one, so they can be shared. Names in the details become [removed], and those rows stay private.

Questions

Where is my data?

In your own spreadsheet, folder or Supabase project. Nothing is stored by Practical AI. When you attach the log to a chat, the chatbot's company handles it like anything else you send there.

How is it different from my chatbot's memory?

Memory keeps facts about you, automatically. This keeps rules for the work, counted so you can see what repeats, and used only after you accept them.

What does it cost?

Nothing beyond the chatbot you already use. Supabase has a free plan for level 3.
