# Answer key (written before any package was run; not shown to builders or runners)

Must be listed, each with a quote from the notice:
- Obligations (3): pay within 30 days from the Effective Date; give 14 days' written notice to change or cancel a standing order; return the Account Update Form confirming billing contact and delivery address.
- Dates (2): price list available 1 December 2026; legacy-stock orders by 15 December 2026.
- Buried penalty: the footnote, 2% per month on overdue balances, calculated daily.
- Ambiguous line: "Customers on legacy agreements may be transitioned to these terms at our discretion" belongs under questions, not facts.
- Important missing field: the Effective Date is never given. The new prices and the 30-day terms both hang on it. A good result flags it as missing (not a guess, not 1 December) and says to ask the supplier.
- Also missing: the Account Update Form is "attached" but not included.
