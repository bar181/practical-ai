# Check sheet

Print this, or copy it into a note beside the chat. It is the part of the tool only you can do.
The AI's quotes are not checked by the AI. They are checked by you, here.

**Think of it as** checking the underlines. For each item in the brief, put your finger on the
words in the document.

## 1. Read the "Watch out" line first

It tells you how many penalties, things to do, dates and questions the brief found. If a number
looks wrong for the document, say so before you check anything else.

## 2. Check each item

For each numbered item in the brief, find its quote at the place it gives. Use your browser's or
reader's find (Ctrl+F, or Cmd+F on a Mac) with three or four words from the quote.

Add rows as needed. An item gets a tick only with three yeses.

**Then look for what is missing.** Skim the document once more for "must", "required", "fee",
"charge", "penalty", "by" and any date. Anything the brief left out is a cross too.

## 3. Fix the crosses

For each cross, paste this into the same chat:

```
<item number> does not match the document: <what you found, in a few words>.
Show me the exact words from the document, or correct the item.
```

Do not act on a crossed item until it has been fixed and you have ticked the new version.
