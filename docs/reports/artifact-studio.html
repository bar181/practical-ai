Artifact Studio, explained

Have rough notes and something to share?

Paste one prompt into the chatbot you already use and get a one-page report your reader can take in on a phone. Then say one sentence, and the same notes come back as a deck or a post.

  • Free and open source
  • No sign-up
  • Nothing to install

Why it matters. "Make this look better" is not a solution. The AI does not know what better means to you. A brief names the job, the reader and the shape.

Make your first report in 5 minutes

Think of it as

AI is a smart intern.

A new hire who has read every book in the world and has never met you. It is fast and capable. It does not yet know your priorities, your tone or what good looks like in your business.

Artifact Studio is the design brief you hand that intern on day one. You bring the content and the judgement. The studio brings the taste.

What it does

What it does: gives your AI what a designer would bring

Artifact Studio is a free, curated library of design templates that turns an AI answer into a slide deck, a report, a web page or a social picture. You paste one link into your chatbot, and the brief inside tells it three things before it builds:

  • A templateWhat kind of thing it is making: a one-page report, a proposal, a landing page, a scrolling explainer, a deck or a social post.
  • A voiceHow it speaks and in what order: for example the analyst, who gives the answer first, or the teacher, who starts with an analogy.
  • A lookWhat it looks like: one of nine looks, or one built from your photo or your brand colour.

It also hands over rules for what good looks like. The main one: it uses only your facts. Where your notes leave something out, it marks the gap instead of guessing.

In the library: 20 templates, 60 components, 9 looks. Every one comes with the sentence that asks for it.

Four ways to use it

Same notes, new shape: four jobs you can give it

Once your report is right, stay in the same chat and say one sentence. Same facts, new shape, new voice.

Use case 1

A monthly report for a partner

Say this
My business partner reads on her phone and wants the answer first. Our colour is green.
You get
A one-page report: the answer first, three key numbers, a chart and what happens next. The worked example above is this use case.
Your part
Confirm the figures and the conclusion.

By hand: 1 to 2 hours. With the studio: about 5 minutes.

Use case 2

A deck for a team meeting

Say this
Show the same notes as a keynote-style slide deck: black slides, one idea each, huge type.
You get
One file you present straight from the browser. Open it, press the arrow keys, go full screen. No PowerPoint, no export. There are four kinds: visionary, story, briefing and workshop.
Your part
Check every number. Decide what to cut.

By hand: 1 hour to several days. With the studio: about 5 minutes to a draft, or about 2 minutes in the same chat as your report.

Use case 3

Posts for the week

Say this
Write three drafts of a LinkedIn post about my notes, in my voice. Take a different way in for each. Show each one as it will look in the feed, side by side. Do not post anything.
You get
Three drafts side by side, each cut where a feed would cut it, so you judge the opening. It works for six places: LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, an HTML email with its inbox line, and a short blog post.
Your part
Pick the draft and post it yourself: nothing is posted for you. For your own voice, paste three posts you are proud of and say "write in this voice".

About 2 minutes in the same chat. Advanced: run the request on a schedule and review the previews over coffee. That needs an assistant that can run tasks on a schedule; the library does not set it up.

Use case 4

A page that opens with your own photo

Say this
Open with my photo as a slow pan and zoom, and set the title in huge letters filled with the photo. Attach the photo first.
You get
A page that opens on your picture. There are thirteen photo effects, such as a sketch that draws itself, a before and after divider and a postcard that flips. Each needs one picture and one sentence, and none needs a library or an app.
Your part
Choose the photo. When it is the page's main picture, its colours can set the look.

About 2 minutes in the same chat.

All times on this page are the project's estimates, not measurements. The point is the shape of the saving: the AI does the layout and the first draft, and you keep the judgement.
