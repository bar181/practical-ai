## 5. The brief

**Decision:** Should Tidy Paws Grooming buy a mobile grooming van in the next twelve months?

**Recommendation:** Don't buy a van now. Run a 12-week pickup-and-drop-off trial for older clients, with a waitlist for a future van, and decide on the van in spring. **Check first:** have your insurance broker confirm cover for carrying client dogs, and call 20 older clients to gauge interest.

**Why**
- Every grooming van I found was over $60,000. The cheapest used one comes to about $62,700 before tax and import costs, and a new one is about $146,800 (estimates at the Bank of Canada rate of 1.4257).
- You can't hire before spring, so a van would have to take a groomer out of a salon. Most of its dogs would be moved, not added, which does little for the 15% sales goal ($93,000).
- The trial reaches older clients now, costs almost nothing, and shows whether there are enough paying "come to me" clients to justify a van.

**SWOT**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Strengths:** serves older clients now; low cost; no hire or lease; groomers stay productive | **Weaknesses:** adds no grooming capacity; uses staff driving time; small sales gain this year |
| **Opportunities:** 91,295 residents aged 65+ in Waterloo Region; the waitlist tests van demand and price; spring hiring | **Threats:** competitors already offer pickup; liability for carrying dogs; uptake may be low |

**Risks and how to reduce them**
1. *Liability:* get cover confirmed in writing before the first pickup, and use crates.
2. *Low uptake:* target clients who have lapsed or who told you transport is hard, and offer the service on set days and in a set area.
3. *Staff strain:* use a non-grooming staff member during their scheduled hours, so no groomer works a sixth day.

**Next steps (next 30 days)**
1. Call your insurance broker and City of Kitchener Licensing (519-741-2275).
2. Call 20 or more older and lapsed clients. Offer pickup, and ask whether they would book a van groom at a stated price, for example salon price plus $30 (**proposal**).
3. Set the trial's days, area, fee (for example $20 round trip, **proposal**) and driver. Announce it in both salons.

**What would change this recommendation:** strong trial results, for example 30 or more paid pickups and 25 or more waitlist names (**proposal**), together with a van that fits $60,000 all-in and a groomer you can hire in spring. If all of these hold, buy the van in spring. The same applies if your $60,000 is cash with financing on top and the other conditions hold.

**Assumptions:** $60,000 is the total investment; "no new lease" means premises; spring is about March 2027; you have a vehicle and a non-grooming staff member who can drive; $120 is a stand-in groom price.
